# 20.1 “Keen Kingfisher”（翠鳥） Series｜20.1 “Keen Kingfisher”（翠鳥）系列

> 章節：[Releases](<000 目錄.md#c-1>) › [Community Edition](<000 目錄.md#c-2>)

For over 5 years now, OPNsense is driving innovation through modularising and hardening the open source firewall, with simple and reliable firmware upgrades, multi-language support, HardenedBSD security, fast adoption of upstream software updates as well as clear and stable 2-Clause BSD licensing.

5 多年來，OPNsense 透過模組化和強化開源防火牆來推動創新，提供簡單可靠的韌體升級、多語言支援、強化的 BSD 安全性、快速採用上游軟體更新以及清晰穩定的 2 條款BSD許可。

20.1, nicknamed “Keen Kingfisher”（翠鳥）, is a subtle improvement on sustainable firewall experience. This release adds VXLAN and additional loopback device support, IPsec public key authentication and elliptic curve TLS certificate creation amongst others. Third party software has been updated to their latest versions. The logging frontend was rewritten for MVC with seamless API support. On the far side the documentation increased in quality as well as quantity and now presents itself in a familiar menu layout.

20.1 （又稱“Keen Kingfisher”（翠鳥） ）是對永續防火牆體驗的細微改進。此版本新增了VXLAN和額外的環回裝置支援、IPsec公鑰認證以及橢圓曲線TLS憑證建立等功能。第三方軟體已更新至最新版本。日誌前端已重寫，以支援MVC ，並無縫相容API 。此外，文件的品質和數量均有所提升，並採用熟悉的選單佈局。

## 20.1 (January 30, 2020)｜20.1 （2020年1月30日）

For over 5 years now, OPNsense is driving innovation through modularising and hardening the open source firewall, with simple and reliable firmware upgrades, multi-language support, HardenedBSD security, fast adoption of upstream software updates as well as clear and stable 2-Clause BSD licensing.

## 20.1.r1 (January 24, 2020)｜20.1 .r1（2020年1月24日）

For over 5 years now, OPNsense is driving innovation through modularising and hardening the open source firewall, with simple and reliable firmware upgrades, multi-language support, HardenedBSD security, fast adoption of upstream software updates as well as clear and stable 2-Clause BSD licensing.

5 多年來，OPNsense 透過模組化和強化開源防火牆來推動創新，提供簡單可靠的韌體升級、多語言支援、強化的 BSD 安全性、快速採用上游軟體更新以及清晰穩定的 2 條款BSD許可。

We thank all of you for helping test, shape and contribute to the project! We know it would not be the same without you.

感謝大家參與測試、完善和貢獻這個專案！我們知道，沒有你們，這個計畫就不會是現在的樣子。
