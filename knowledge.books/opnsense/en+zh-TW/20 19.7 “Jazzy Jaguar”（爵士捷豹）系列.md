# 19.7 “Jazzy Jaguar”（爵士捷豹） Series｜19.7 “Jazzy Jaguar”（爵士捷豹）系列

> 章節：[Releases](<000 目錄.md#c-1>) › [Community Edition](<000 目錄.md#c-2>)

For four and a half years now, OPNsense is driving innovation through modularising and hardening the open source firewall, with simple and reliable firmware upgrades, multi-language support, HardenedBSD security, fast adoption of upstream software updates as well as clear and stable 2-Clause BSD licensing.

四年半以來，OPNsense 透過模組化和強化開源防火牆來推動創新，提供簡單可靠的韌體升級、多語言支援、強化的 BSD 安全性、快速採用上游軟體更新以及清晰穩定的 2 條款BSD許可。

19.7, nicknamed “Jazzy Jaguar”（爵士捷豹）, embodies an iteration of what should be considered enjoyable user experience for firewalls in general: improved statistics and visibility of rules, reliable and consistent live logging and alias utility improvements. Apart from the usual upgrades of third party software to up-to-date releases, OPNsense now also offers built-in remote system logging through Syslog-ng, route-based IPsec, updated translations with Spanish as a brand new and already fully translated language and newer Netmap code with VirtIO, VLAN child and vmxnet support.

19.7 （又稱“Jazzy Jaguar”（爵士捷豹） ）體現了防火牆使用者體驗的全新迭代：改進了規則的統計資訊和可見性，增強了即時日誌記錄的可靠性和一致性，並改進了別名工具。除了將第三方軟體升級到最新版本外，OPNsense 現在還提供透過 Syslog-ng 實現的內建遠端系統日誌記錄、基於路由的 IPsec、更新的翻譯（新增西班牙語，且已完成翻譯）以及支援 VirtIO、 VLAN子節點和 vmxnet 的更新版 Netmap 程式碼。

Last but not least we would like to thank m.a.x. it for their sponsorship of the default gateway priority switching feature and their continued work of writing and maintaining plenty of community plugins. This time around, Maltrail, Netdata and WireGuard VPN have been freshly added to the mix.

最後，我們要感謝 Max It 對預設閘道優先權切換功能的贊助，以及他們持續編寫和維護大量社群插件的工作。此次，Maltrail、Netdata 和 WireGuard VPN也加入了支援行列。

## 19.7.r1 (July 09, 2019)｜19.7 .r1（2019年7月9日）

For four and a half years now, OPNsense is driving innovation through modularising and hardening the open source firewall, with simple and reliable firmware upgrades, multi-language support, HardenedBSD security, fast adoption of upstream software updates as well as clear and stable 2-Clause BSD licensing.

四年半以來，OPNsense 透過模組化和強化開源防火牆，推動創新，提供簡單可靠的韌體升級、多語言支援、強化的 BSD 安全性、快速採用上游軟體更新以及清晰穩定的 2 條款BSD許可。

We thank all of you for helping test, shape and contribute to the project! We know it would not be the same without you.

Known issues and limitations:

已知問題和限制：

-   Filterlog spamming console due to new Syslog-ng integration. Temporary workaround is stopping filterlog via “pkill filterlog”.  
    由於新的 Syslog-ng 集成，Filterlog 日誌會大量輸出到控制台。臨時解決方法是使用“pkill filterlog”命令停止 Filterlog 日誌輸出。
    
-   OpenVPN no longer supports listening on gateway groups. Use localhost paired with port forwards instead.  
    OpenVPN 已不再支援監聽網關群組。請改用 localhost 並配合連接埠轉送。
    
-   The web proxy login privilege is no longer available. Access may be restricted by a group selector instead.  
    Web代理登入權限已不再可用。存取權限可能改為透過群組選擇器進行限制。
    
-   Web proxy squid update from version 3 to 4 breaks the cache database. To repair go to “Services: Web Proxy: Administration” tab “Support”（支援） and click “Reset”（重置）.  
    Web 代理程式 Squid 從版本 3 更新到版本 4 會破壞快取資料庫。若要修復此問題，請前往「服務：Web 代理程式：管理」標籤“Support”（支援）並按一下“Reset”（重置） 。
    
-   Nano images require a reinstall using the latest image to avoid inode shortage which makes the system appear to run out of space during recent 19.1.x updates.  
    Nano 鏡像需要使用最新鏡像重新安裝，以避免 inode 短缺導致系統在最近的19.1 .x 更新期間出現空間不足的情況。
