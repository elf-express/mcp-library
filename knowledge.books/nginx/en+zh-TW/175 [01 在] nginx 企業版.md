#### Corporate Sponsors and Ownership｜企業贊助商和所有權

[F5, Inc.](https://www.f5.com/) is the primary maintainer, sponsor, and steward of NGINX since the [acquisition of NGINX, Inc. in 2019](https://www.f5.com/company/news/press-releases/f5-acquires-nginx-to-bridge-netops-devops). Learn more about F5's [stewardship and governance](<039 [01.01 文件] NGINX開源治理.md>) of NGINX.

[F5, Inc.](https://www.f5.com/)自 [2019 年收購NGINX , Inc.](https://www.f5.com/company/news/press-releases/f5-acquires-nginx-to-bridge-netops-devops)以來，一直是NGINX的主要維護者、發起人和管理者。了解更多關於 F5 對NGINX的 [管理與治理](<039 [01.01 文件] NGINX開源治理.md>)的資訊。

NGINX® is a registered trademark of F5, Inc.

NGINX ® 是 F5, Inc. 的註冊商標。

#### Enterprise Distributions｜企業發行

[F5](https://www.f5.com/products#f5-nginx) provides enterprise distributions of NGINX, including [NGINX Plus](https://www.f5.com/products/nginx/nginx-plus).

[F5](https://www.f5.com/products#f5-nginx)提供NGINX的企業發行版，包括 [NGINX Plus](https://www.f5.com/products/nginx/nginx-plus) 。

#### Commercial Support｜商業支持

Commercial support for NGINX is available with the [Open Source Subscription Bundles](https://www.f5.com/products/nginx/open-source-subscription-bundles) from F5.

F5 提供的 [開源訂閱包](https://www.f5.com/products/nginx/open-source-subscription-bundles)可為NGINX提供商業支援。

#### Training and Certification｜培訓和認證

NGINX training is available from [F5](https://www.f5.com/learn/training#f:@f5_primary_product=%5BNGINX%5D).

NGINX訓練可從 [F5](https://www.f5.com/learn/training#f:@f5_primary_product=%5BNGINX%5D)開始。
