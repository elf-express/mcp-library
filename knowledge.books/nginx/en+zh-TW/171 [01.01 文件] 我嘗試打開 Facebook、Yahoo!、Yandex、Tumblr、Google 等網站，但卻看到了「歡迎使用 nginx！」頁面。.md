## I am trying to open Facebook, Yahoo!, Yandex, Tumblr, Google etc., and instead I am getting “Welcome to nginx!” page｜我嘗試打開 Facebook、Yahoo!、Yandex、Tumblr、Google 等網站，但卻看到了「歡迎使用 nginx！」頁面。

**Q:** I am trying to open Facebook, Yahoo!, Yandex, Google, or some other well known web site and instead I am getting a blank web page with a message referring to nginx: “Welcome to nginx!” or “404 Not Found / nginx”.

**問：** 我正在嘗試打開 Facebook、Yahoo!、Yandex、Google 或其他一些知名網站，但我得到的是一個空白網頁，其中包含一條涉及 nginx 的消息：“歡迎使用 nginx！”或“404 未找到/nginx”。

I suspect something is wrong and there is probably a malicious attempt to direct me to a rogue web page (to break into my computer, do phishing etc.). Why is that, and what has nginx to do with my attempts to connect to Facebook (Yahoo!, Google, etc.) ?

我懷疑出了問題，很可能有人惡意試圖將我引導到一個惡意網頁（例如入侵我的電腦、進行網路釣魚等）。這是為什麼呢？ nginx 與我嘗試連結 Facebook（Yahoo、Google等）又有什麼關係呢？

**A:** First of all, the “Welcome to nginx!” page you see is NOT our website. At nginx, we write and distribute a **free** [open source](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software) web server software. A web page saying “Welcome to nginx!” is just a diagnostics response that can be produced by any of the websites out there, running nginx web server. Currently, nginx is the 2nd most popular open source web server in the world, it’s being used by over 126,000,000 (or 14% of the Internet) websites. Most of these websites are legitimate, but some aren’t. Our software was created with a good reason of enabling performance and scalability on the Internet, it is licensed under [popular open source license](<354 頁.md>), and has nothing to do with any kind of threatening or malicious activity per se — nginx is NOT a malware, and it is NOT on your computer. But someone’s malware could have indeed tampered with your computer or router, redirecting you to a fraudulent Internet server.

**答：** 首先，您看到的「歡迎使用 nginx！」頁面是 NOT 我們的網站。在 nginx，我們編寫並分發**免費**的[開源]。(http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_software) 網路伺服器軟體。任何經營 nginx 網路伺服器的網站，只要看到「歡迎使用 nginx！」這樣的網頁，都只是一個簡單的診斷回應。目前，nginx 是全球第二大最受歡迎的開源網路伺服器，已被超過 1.26 億個網站（佔網路網站的 14%）使用。這些網站大多合法，但也有一些不合法。我們的軟體旨在提升網路的效能和可擴展性，並採用[流行的開源許可證]授權。(http://nginx.org/LICENSE)並且與任何威脅或惡意活動本身無關——nginx 是 NOT 它是一種惡意軟體，而且是 NOT 在你的電腦上。但確實有可能有人植入了惡意軟體，篡改了你的電腦或路由器，將你重定向到一個欺詐性的網路伺服器。

We recommend running an anti-virus check on your computer, and we recommend to check and verify your entire system setup with the help of your ISP, or another support personnel:

我們建議您對電腦進行防毒掃描，並建議您在ISP或其他支援人員的協助下檢查和驗證您的整個系統設定：

(Disclaimer: at nginx we are not responsible for any negative impact or effects that the actions below might cause. Use the following recommendations at your own risk, especially if you aren’t an experienced user of your operating system and/or Internet applications. In no event shall nginx be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, or consequential damages, including, but not limited to loss of use, data, or profits; or business interruption).

（免責聲明：nginx不對以下操作可能造成的任何負面影響或後果負責。請自行承擔使用以下建議的風險，尤其是在您不熟悉作業系統和/或網路應用程式的情況下。在任何情況下，nginx均不對任何直接、間接、附帶、特殊、懲罰性或後果性損害承擔責任，包括但不限於使用損失、資料中斷或利潤損失。

-   Check your TCP/IP settings and see if the DNS servers configuration matches the valid one (suggested by your Internet service provider or IT support personnel).  
    檢查您的TCP/IP設置，並查看DNS伺服器配置是否與有效配置（由您的網路服務供應商或IT支援人員建議）相符。
-   Use [Google Public DNS](http://developers.google.com/speed/public-dns/), and see if it fixes the problem. From Google’s description of its Public DNS — "Google Public DNS is a free, global Domain Name System (DNS) resolution service, that you can use as an alternative to your current DNS provider. \[..\] By using Google Public DNS you can: Speed up your browsing experience. **Improve your security**."  
    請使用 [Google 公共 DNS](http://developers.google.com/speed/public-dns/) ，看看能否解決問題。 Google 對其公共 DNS 的描述如下：“Google 公共 DNS 是一項免費的全球域名系統 (DNS) 解析服務，您可以將其用作當前 DNS 提供商的替代方案。[...] 使用 Google 公共 DNS，您可以：加快瀏覽速度，**提高安全性**。”
-   Clear your DNS resolver cache. On Microsoft Windows XP go to Start > Run, and then type the following command: "ipconfig /flushdns". On Microsoft Vista, Windows 7, and Windows 8 click on Start logo, follow All Programs > Accessories, right-click on Command Prompt, choose "Run As Administrator"（以管理員身份執行）, type in "ipconfig /flushdns" and hit Enter.  
    清除DNS快取。在 Microsoft Windows XP中，依序點選“開始”>“執行”，然後輸入以下指令：“ipconfig /flushdns”。在 Microsoft Vista、Windows 7 和 Windows 8 系統中，點擊“開始”圖標，依序點擊“所有程式”>“附件”，右鍵點擊“命令"Run As Administrator"（以管理員身份執行） ”，選擇“ipconfig /flushdns”，然後按 Enter 鍵。
-   Click the "page reload" button in your browser. Clear browser data (cache, cookies etc.). E.g. with Chrome find and click "Clear Browsing Data"（清除瀏覽數據） (Settings > Under the Hood). With Internet Explorer find Tools > Internet Options > General. **Caution:** you may be deleting saved passwords information here, so do it carefully and check what exact actions you are performing.  
    點選瀏覽器中的「頁面刷新」按鈕。清除瀏覽器資料（快取、Cookie 等）。例如，在 Chrome 瀏覽器中，找到並點擊"Clear Browsing Data"（清除瀏覽數據）設定」>「進階設定」。在 Internet Explorer 瀏覽器中，找到「工具」>「Internet 選項」>「常規」。 **注意：**您可能會在此處刪除已儲存的密碼訊息，因此請謹慎操作，並仔細檢查您正在執行的特定操作。
-   Check if the "hosts" file doesn’t contain entries other than "127.0.0.1 localhost", and if so — if these entries are for the web site you’re trying to reach. The "hosts" files is located in C:\\WINDOWS\\system32\\drivers\\etc directory. Typically there should be just one entry in it, for "127.0.0.1 localhost", that’s it. The "hosts" file can be viewed and edited with your standard Notepad application.  
    檢查「hosts」檔案中是否除了「 127.0.0.1 」之外還有其他項目，如果有，請確認這些項目是否指​​向您嘗試造訪的網站。 “hosts”檔案位於C:\\\\ WINDOWS \\drivers\\etc目錄下。通常情況下，其中應該只有一個條目，即「 127.0.0.1 」。您可以使用記事本檢視和編輯「hosts」檔案。
-   Check the plugins and extensions installed with your browser. Re-install your browser or try an alternative one if possible.  
    檢查瀏覽器安裝的插件和擴充功能。如果可以，請重新安裝瀏覽器或嘗試使用其他瀏覽器。

Something must be wrong with your **operating system** settings, **home router** setup, or **browser** configuration, if you are trying to access a well known web site and what you get instead is “Welcome to nginx!”. This should NOT happen if your computers and network are clean and safe.

如果您嘗試訪問一個眾所周知的網站，而您得到的卻是“歡迎來到 nginx！”，那麼您的**作業系統**設定、**家庭路由器**設定或**瀏覽器**配置一定有問題。如果您的電腦和網路乾淨且安全，這應該會發生NOT。

If changing DNS servers to Google Public DNS, flushing DNS resolver cache, fixing your browser configuration, or cleaning "hosts" file (when applicable) have helped, it might be that there’s a malware somewhere on your PC or around. Find and clean it using your preferred anti-virus and anti-malware tools.

如果將DNS變更為 Google 公用DNS 、清除DNS快取、修復瀏覽器設定或清理 hosts 檔案（如適用）後問題已解決，則可能是您的PC或周圍存在惡意軟體。請使用您常用的防毒和反惡意軟體工具來尋找並清除它。
