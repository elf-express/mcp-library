## Command-line interface｜命令列介面

njs scripts development and debugging can be performed from the command-line. The command-line utility is available after the installation of the Linux [package](<134 [01.01.05 紐澤西州] 下載並安裝.md#install_package>) or after building from the [sources](<134 [01.01.05 紐澤西州] 下載並安裝.md#install_sources>). Compared to njs running inside nginx, nginx objects ([HTTP](<137 [01.01.05 紐澤西州] 參考.md#http>) and [Stream](<137 [01.01.05 紐澤西州] 參考.md#stream>)) are not available in the utility.

njs 腳本的開發和偵錯可以透過命令列完成。安裝 Linux 軟體包(https://nginx.org/en/docs/njs/install.html#install_package)或從原始碼(https://nginx.org/en/docs/njs/install.html#install_sources)建置後，即可使用此命令列工具。與在 nginx 中運行的 njs 相比，該工具不支援 nginx 物件（[HTTP](<137 [01.01.05 紐澤西州] 參考.md#http>)和 [Stream](<137 [01.01.05 紐澤西州] 參考.md#stream>) ）。
