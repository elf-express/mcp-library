> 章節：[en](<000 目錄.md#c-1>) › [docs](<000 目錄.md#c-2>) › [njs](<000 目錄.md#c-7>)

## Command-line interface

njs scripts development and debugging can be performed from the command-line. The command-line utility is available after the installation of the Linux [package](<134 [01.01.05 njs] Download and install.md#install_package>) or after building from the [sources](<134 [01.01.05 njs] Download and install.md#install_sources>). Compared to njs running inside nginx, nginx objects ([HTTP](<137 [01.01.05 njs] Reference.md#http>) and [Stream](<137 [01.01.05 njs] Reference.md#stream>)) are not available in the utility.

> ```bash
> $ echo "2**3" | njs -q
> 8
> 
> $ njs

> global {

>    NGINX_VERSION: '1.17.9',
