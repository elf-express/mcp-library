## nginx: community｜Nginx：社區

#### Community and Governance｜社區與治理

Open source powers much of the technology world. NGINX has been a strong part of this movement from its inception. As the internet has grown, so have we, from our initial web server to the myriad of projects involving JavaScript, Kubernetes, API gateways, OpenTelemetry, and WebAssembly. NGINX is committed to open source both in ethos and practicality, aiming to deliver reliable and cutting-edge technology within a healthy open source culture. Whether NGINX is part of your open source past or a step in your present, we welcome you into the future together.

開源為科技世界的大部分提供了動力。從一開始，NGINX 就一直是這項運動的重要組成部分。隨著網際網路的發展，我們也從最初的 Web 伺服器發展到涉及 JavaScript、Kubernetes、API 閘道、OpenTelemetry 和 WebAssembly 的無數專案。 NGINX 致力於開源精神和實用性，旨在在健康的開源文化中提供可靠和尖端的技術。無論NGINX是您開源過去的一部分還是現在的一步，我們歡迎您一起走向未來。

To learn more about our commitment, please visit our [Governance](<039 [01.01 文件] NGINX開源治理.md>) page.

要了解有關我們承諾的更多信息，請訪問我們的[治理](<039 [01.01 文件] NGINX開源治理.md>)頁面。

#### Committed to Our Mission｜致力於我們的使命

We believe that healthy communities are core to innovation and open source sustainability. It is our mission to enable the NGINX community to engage with and develop solutions based on open and available NGINX technologies. Together we can power the future.

我們相信健康的社區是創新和開源永續發展的核心。我們的使命是使NGINX社區能夠參與並開發基於開放和可用的NGINX技術的解決方案。我們可以共同為未來提供動力。

Our community is your entry into an open world of information, collaboration, and discussion. Thanks for joining us.

我們的社群是您進入資訊、協作和討論的開放世界的入口。感謝您加入我們。

#### Code of Conduct｜行為準則

Per our values, NGINX strives to create an inclusive community for all. Please remember our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/nginx/nginx/blob/master/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) (CoC) when participating in the NGINX community.

根據我們的價值觀，NGINX 致力於為所有人創造一個包容性的社區。參與NGINX社群時請記住我們的[行為準則](https://github.com/nginx/nginx/blob/master/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)（CoC）。

If you wish to tell us about a CoC violation, please email us here: [nginx-oss-community@f5.com](mailto://nginx-oss-community@f5.com)

如果您想告訴我們有關 CoC 違規的情況，請在此處給我們發送電子郵件：[nginx-oss-community@f5.com](mailto://nginx-oss-community@f5.com)
