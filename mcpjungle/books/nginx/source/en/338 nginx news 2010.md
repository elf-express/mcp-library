| 2010-07-28 | [nginx-0.8.47](<174 [01 en] nginx download.md>) development version has been released.<br>W3Techs reports that [5.1% of the top 1 million sites on the web](http://w3techs.com/technologies/overview/web_server/all) (according to Alexa) use nginx. Hence nginx is on the 3rd place after Apache (70.2%) and IIS (20.5%).<br>By the way, Netcraft reports similar [5.21% nginx share of the million busiest sites in July 2010](http://news.netcraft.com/archives/2010/07/16/july-2010-web-server-survey-16.html).<br>Another survey by BuiltWith.com. It’s interesting that nginx is used rather on more loaded sites: [4.95% of the top 10,000 sites versus 3.14% of the top million](http://trends.builtwith.com/Web%20Server/nginx) (on July 27, 2010), while Apache is used more on less loaded sites: [63.60% of the top million sites versus 55.79% of the top 10,000 sites](http://trends.builtwith.com/Web-Server/Apache). IIS/6.0 is more preferred on middle range sites: [24.49% of the top 100,000 sites versus 20.05% of the top 10,000 and 21.15% of the top million](http://trends.builtwith.com/Web-Server/IIS-6).<br>Also two [books](<021 [01 en] nginx books.md>) about nginx have recently been published: “[实战Nginx：取代Apache的高性能Web服务器](http://product.dangdang.com/product.aspx?product_id=20807089)” by [张宴 (Zhang Yan)](http://blog.s135.com/) in Chinese and “[Nginx HTTP Server](http://www.packtpub.com/nginx-http-server-for-web-applications/book)” by [Clément Nedelcu](http://cnedelcu.blogspot.com/) in English. |
