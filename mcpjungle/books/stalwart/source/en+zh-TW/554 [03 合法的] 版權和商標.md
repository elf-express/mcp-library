If you are a rightsholder \(or an agent authorised to act on a rightsholder’s behalf\) and you believe that content on the Support Portal at **support.stalw.art, on the documentation or blog at**stalw.art, or on any other surface operated by Stalwart Labs Ltd infringes your copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property right, you may submit a notice using the procedure below. We aim to respond to valid notices promptly.

 Stalwart Labs Ltd 尊重他人的智慧財產權。如果您是權利持有者（或被授權代表權利持有者行事的代理人），並且您認為支援入口網站（**support.stalw.art）、文件或部落格（**stalw.art）或 Stalwart Labs Ltd 營運的任何其他表面上的內容侵犯了您的版權、商標或其他智慧財產權，您可以使用以下程式提交通知。我們的目標是及時回覆有效通知。

### What your notice must include｜您的通知必須包括哪些內容

 To be actionable, your notice must include all of the following:

 為了具有可操作性，您的通知必須包含以下所有內容：

1. **Identification of the rightsholder.** Your full name, the name of the entity you represent \(if any\), your postal address, telephone number, and email address.
**權利持有人的身分資訊。** 您的全名、您所代表的實體的名稱（如果有）、您的郵寄地址、電話號碼和電子郵件地址。
2. **Identification of the right.** A description of the copyrighted work, registered trademark, or other right that you claim has been infringed \(for trademarks, please include the registration number and territory\).
**權利識別。** 您聲稱受到侵犯的版權作品、註冊商標或其他權利的描述（對於商標，請包括註冊號碼和地區）。
3. **Identification of the allegedly infringing content.** A URL or other location identifier sufficient for us to find the material on our systems, with a brief description of how it allegedly infringes.
**涉嫌侵權內容的識別。** URL 或其他位置標識符足以讓我們在我們的系統上找到該資料，並簡要描述其涉嫌侵權的方式。
4. **A good-faith statement.** A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorised by the rightsholder, its agent, or applicable law.
**善意聲明。** 聲明您善意地相信該資料的使用未經權利持有者、其代理人或適用法律的授權。
5. **An accuracy statement under penalty of perjury\(where the notice is submitted under U.S. law\) or**a statement of accuracy \(where the notice is submitted under UK or EU law\). The signatory represents that the information in the notice is accurate, and that they are authorised to act on the rightsholder’s behalf.
**受偽證處罰的準確性聲明（如果根據美國法律提交通知）或**準確性聲明（如果根據UK或EU法律提交通知）。簽字人聲明通知中的資訊準確無誤，並且他們被授權代表權利人行事。
6\. **A signature.** A physical or electronic signature.
**簽名。** 實體或電子簽名。
Incomplete notices may be returned for completion and may not be acted on until a complete notice is received.

 不完整的通知可能會被退回以供填寫，並且在收到完整的通知之前可能不會採取行動。

### What we will do on receipt of a valid notice｜收到有效通知後我們將做什麼

 On receipt of a notice that, on its face, contains the elements above, we will:

 收到表面上包含上述要素的通知後，我們將：

1. Promptly assess the notice for completeness and apparent validity.
立即評估通知的完整性和明顯有效性。
2. Where appropriate, remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing content, or restrict its visibility, pending further review.
在適當的情況下，刪除或禁止存取涉嫌侵權的內容，或限制其可見性，等待進一步審查。
3. Notify the user who posted the content \(if applicable\) of the action taken and provide them with a copy of the notice \(with personal contact details redacted on request and where reasonably practicable\).
通知發佈內容（如果適用）的使用者所採取的措施，並向他們提供通知副本（根據要求並在合理可行的情況下，編輯個人聯絡資訊）。
4. Maintain a record of the notice, the action taken, and any subsequent counter-notice for our internal compliance purposes.
出於我們內部合規的目的，保留通知、採取的行動以及任何後續反通知的記錄。
### Counter-notice｜反通知

 If you are the user whose content has been removed and you believe in good faith that the removal was the result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice to **[\\[email protected\\]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#ddbcbfa8aeb89daea9bcb1aaf3bcafa9)**. The counter-notice must include:

 如果您是內容已刪除的用戶，並且您真誠地認為該刪除是由於錯誤或錯誤識別造成的，您可以向 **[\\[email protected\\]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#ddbcbfa8aeb89daea9bcb1aaf3bcafa9)** 提交反通知。反通知必須包括：

1. Your full name, postal address, telephone number, and email address.
您的全名、郵寄地址、電話號碼和電子郵件地址。
2. Identification of the content that was removed and the location at which it appeared before removal.
識別已刪除的內容及其在刪除之前出現的位置。
3. A statement, under penalty of perjury or as a statement of accuracy under UK or EU law as applicable, that you have a good-faith belief that the content was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
一份聲明，表明您善意地相信內容因失誤或誤認而被刪除，否則將受偽證罪處罰，或作為UK或EU適用法律的準確性聲明。
4. Your consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales \(and, if you reside in the United States, your additional consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for the judicial district in which your address is located, or, if your address is outside the United States, the United States District Court for the District of Wyoming\) for any judicial proceeding arising from the dispute.
您同意英格蘭和威爾斯法院對爭議引起的任何司法程序具有管轄權（如果您居住在美國，也同意您地址所在司法區的聯邦法院的管轄權，或者，如果您的地址在美國境外，則同意美國懷俄明州地區法院的管轄權）。
5. A physical or electronic signature.
實體或電子簽名。
On receipt of a valid counter-notice, we will forward a copy to the original notifier. Unless we receive notice within ten \(10\) business days that the original notifier has filed a court action seeking to restrain the user from further infringement, we may, at our discretion, restore the removed content.
 收到有效的反通知後，我們會將副本轉發給原始通知人。除非我們在十 \(10\) 個工作天內收到通知，表明原始通知者已向法院提起訴訟，尋求阻止用戶進一步侵權，否則我們可以自行決定恢復已刪除的內容。

### Repeat infringers｜重複侵權者

 We may, at our sole discretion, suspend or terminate the accounts of users who, in our reasonable judgement, are repeat infringers. The contractual basis for suspension and termination is set out in the Terms and Conditions and the Acceptable Use Policy.

 我們可以自行決定暫停或終止經我們合理判斷為屢次侵權的用戶的帳戶。暫停和終止的合約依據在條款和條件和可接受的使用政策中規定。

### Misuse of this procedure｜濫用此程序

 Submitting a knowingly false, misleading, or abusive notice may give rise to liability under applicable law \(for example, under section 512\(f\) of the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act and equivalent provisions of UK and EU law\). Notices used to suppress legitimate criticism, fair comment, or lawful use of a work will not be acted on.

 提交故意虛假、誤導或濫用的通知可能會導致根據適用法律承擔責任（例如，根據美國數字千禧年版權法第 512\(f\) 條以及UK 和 EU 法律的同等條款）。用於壓制合法批評、公正評論或合法使用作品的通知將不會被採取行動。
