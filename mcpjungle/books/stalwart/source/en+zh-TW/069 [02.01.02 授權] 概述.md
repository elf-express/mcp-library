# Overview｜概述

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[auth｜認證\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-3&gt;\\\) › \\\[authorization｜授權\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-5&gt;\\\)

Authorization determines what actions an authenticated user or entity may perform within the server. Where authentication establishes identity, authorization controls what that identity is allowed to do once verified.

 授權決定了已認證的使用者或實體可以在伺服器內執行哪些操作。其中，authentication用於建立身份，而授權則控制該身份在驗證後可以執行哪些操作。

 Stalwart supports fine-grained access control. Permissions can be assigned at several levels: directly to individuals or groups, through roles, or at the tenant level for multi-tenant deployments. The combination of these layers determines the effective permissions of a given principal.

 Stalwart 支援細粒度的存取控制。 客戶可以分配到多個層級：直接指派給個人或群組 ，透過角色分配，或在多權限部署中指派給租用戶層級。這些層級的組合決定了給定主體的有效權限。

 The sections that follow cover the available permission types and how they are configured to match a deployment’s access policy.

 以下各節將介紹可用的權限類型以及如何設定它們以符合部署的存取策略。
