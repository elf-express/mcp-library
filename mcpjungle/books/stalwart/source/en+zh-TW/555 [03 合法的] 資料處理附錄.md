# Data Processing Addendum｜資料處理附錄

> 章節：\\\[legal｜合法的\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-79&gt;\\\)

A Data Processing Addendum \(DPA\) is available on request to Enterprise customers and to customers of the forthcoming Managed Email service \(planned launch in 2027\) where Stalwart Labs Ltd processes personal data of EU or UK data subjects on the customer’s behalf.

 企業客戶和即將推出的託管電子郵件服務（計劃於 2027 年推出）的客戶可根據要求提供資料處理附錄 \(DPA\)，其中 Stalwart Labs Ltd 代表客戶處理EU 或 UK 資料主體的個人資料。

 For the open-source self-hosted server, the customer remains the sole controller of any personal data stored in their own deployment; Stalwart Labs Ltd does not act as a processor and no DPA is required.

 對於開啟源自託管伺服器，客戶仍然是自己部署中儲存的任何個人資料的唯一控制者； Stalwart Labs Ltd 不充當處理者，且不需要 DPA。

 For data that Stalwart Labs Ltd collects directly from individuals through its own websites, including the Licensing Portal at **license.stalw.art**and the Support Portal at**support.stalw.art**, Stalwart Labs Ltd acts as the controller, and that processing is governed by the Privacy Policy rather than by this DPA.

 對於 Stalwart Labs Ltd 透過其自己的網站（包括許可入口網站 **license.stalw.art**和支援入口網站**support.stalw.art**）直接從個人收集的數據，Stalwart Labs Ltd 充當控制者，並且該處理受 隱私權政策 而不是本 DPA 管轄。

## Controller / processor relationship｜控制器/处理器关系

 For Enterprise license holders and Managed Email subscribers, the relationship is as follows:

 對於企業許可證持有者和託管電子郵件訂閱者，關係如下：

- The customer \(or the customer’s organisation\) is the controller of the personal data.
客戶（或客戶的組織）是個人資料的控制者。
- Stalwart Labs Ltd is the processor, acting only on the documented instructions of the controller.
Stalwart Labs Ltd 是處理者，僅依照控制者記錄的指示行事。
- The DPA is supplementary to the Terms and Conditions and incorporates by reference: \(a\) the obligations of processors under Article 28 of the EU GDPR and the UK GDPR; \(b\) the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses \(SCCs\) for transfers of personal data to third countries, where applicable; and \(c\) the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the SCCs, where applicable.
的 DPA 是對條款和條件的補充 並透過引用納入： \(a\) 處理者依據《公約》第 28 條所承擔的義務 EU GDPR 和 UK GDPR; \(b\) 歐盟委員會關於向第三國傳輸個人資料的標準合約條款 \(SCC\)（如適用）； \(c\) UK SCC 的國際資料傳輸附錄（如果適用）。
- The DPA records the categories of data subjects and personal data processed, the duration of processing, the technical and organisational security measures in place, and the list of authorised sub-processors \(see Sub-processor list\).
DPA記錄了處理的資料主體和個人資料的類別、處理的持續時間、採取的技術和組織安全措施以及授權的子處理者清單（請參閱子處理者清單）。
