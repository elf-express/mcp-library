# Overview｜概述

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[mta｜捷運\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-47&gt;\\\) › \\\[rewrite｜改寫\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-54&gt;\\\)

Message rewriting alters aspects of an email message as it is processed by the mail server. This can involve changing the sender or recipient addresses in the envelope \(the ‘outside’ of the message\), or modifying the content itself, including headers, body, and attachments.

 郵件重寫會在郵件伺服器處理電子郵件時改變郵件的某些方面。這可能包括更改信封（郵件的「外部」）中的寄件者或收件者地址，或修改郵件內容本身，包括郵件頭、郵件正文和附件。

 Typical reasons for rewriting a message include changing recipient addresses to redirect mail traffic, obfuscating the original sender’s address for privacy, or altering the domain in an address to match an organisation’s branding. Modifying the content of a message is useful for managing how it is presented to the recipient: operators may add, delete, or change headers for tracking or compliance, alter the body to append a disclaimer or signature, or manage attachments for security, for example by removing potentially harmful files.

 重寫郵件的常見原因包括：更改收件者地址（ \([https://stalw.art/docs/mta/rewrite/address](https://stalw.art/docs/mta/rewrite/address)\) ）以重定向郵件流量、為了保護隱私而混淆原始寄件者地址，或更改地址中的網域名稱以符合組織的品牌識別。修改郵件內容有助於管理郵件呈現給收件人的方式：操作員可以添加、刪除或更改郵件頭\([https://stalw.art/docs/mta/rewrite/headers](https://stalw.art/docs/mta/rewrite/headers)\) ）以進行跟踪或合規性檢查，更改郵件正文\([https://stalw.art/docs/mta/rewrite/content](https://stalw.art/docs/mta/rewrite/content)\) ）以添加免責聲明或簽名，或出於安全考慮管理附件，例如刪除潛在的有害文件。

 Stalwart supports both address rewriting and content modification.

 Stalwart 同時支援位址重寫和內容修改。
