 Available 2027 We are taking expressions of interest now; the service goes live in 2027.

 2027年上線 我們現在接受意向申請；該服務將於2027年正式上線。

 Managed service

 託管服務

## Run Stalwart without running Stalwart｜不運行 Stalwart 運行 Stalwart

 We host, cluster and operate the same mail and collaboration server we ship to self-hosters \(JMAP, IMAP, POP3, SMTP, CalDAV, CardDAV, WebDAV\) on infrastructure we maintain, with included migration help and contractual support.

 我們託管、叢集和營運與提供給自託管使用者相同的郵件和協作伺服器（ JMAP, IMAP, POP3, SMTP 、CalDAV、CardDAV、WebDAV），這些伺服器託管在我們維護的基礎設施上，並提供遷移協助和合約支援。

 Register interest

 What you get

 你將獲得什麼

## A managed Stalwart deployment, sized to your mailbox count.｜根據您的郵箱數量，提供託管的 Stalwart 部署方案。

 Each tenant runs on a Stalwart cluster operated by Stalwart Labs. Provisioning, upgrades, certificate renewal, backups, monitoring and incident response are handled by us. You keep full administrative control over accounts, domains, mail rules, calendars and contacts through the standard Stalwart web admin and CLI. The deployment uses the same code path that runs in self-hosted production. There is no forked feature set; new releases land here on the same cadence as the open-source and Enterprise editions.

 每個租戶都運行在由 Stalwart Labs 營運的 Stalwart 叢集上。配置、升級、憑證續約、備份、監控和事件回應均由我們負責。您可以透過標準的 Stalwart Web 管理介面和CLI完全掌控帳戶、網域、郵件規則、行事曆和聯絡人。部署使用與自託管生產環境相同的程式碼路徑。沒有分支功能集；新版本發布與開源版和企業版保持一致。

 信箱層級

 Tiers are organised by mailbox count and storage allotment. Pricing follows the same per-mailbox bands published on the pricing page for the Enterprise edition, with hosting and operations bundled in. Custom tiers, including high-density hosting providers and multi- tenant deployments, are quoted on request. Every plan includes:

 套餐等級依郵箱數量和儲存空間劃分。定價遵循企業版定價頁面上公佈的單郵箱價格區間，並包含託管和運維服務。客製化套餐（包括高密度託管服務和多租戶部署）可根據需求報價。每個套餐均包含：

- JMAP, IMAP4rev2, POP3 and SMTP submission for clients.
JMAP 、IMAP4rev2、 POP3和SMTP提交給客戶。
- CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV \(plus the JMAP equivalents\).
CalDAV、CardDAV 和 WebDAV（以及JMAP等效項）。

管理 Sieve 和 Sieve 腳本。
- The full anti-spam pipeline, including the statistical classifier, DNSBLs, Pyzor and phishing protection.
完整的反垃圾郵件流程，包括統計分類器、DNSBL、Pyzor 和網路釣魚防護。
- Autoconfig and Autodiscover so end-user clients set themselves up.
自動配置和自動發現功能，使最終用戶用戶端能夠自行完成設定。

Web 管理員、帳戶自助服務入口網站和CLI存取。
Regions
 地區

## Hosted in the region you choose.｜託管於您選擇的地區。

 We are launching in multiple regions across Europe and North America. Specific data-centre locations and providers will be announced ahead of general availability. Customers select the region at sign-up; data residency stays in the chosen region for the lifetime of the account. If you have a specific compliance constraint \(for example a particular member-state location or a sovereign-cloud provider\), tell us at the interest stage and we will factor it into the launch plan.

 我們將在歐洲和北美多個地區推出服務。具體的資料中心位置和提供者將在正式上線前公佈。客戶在註冊時選擇區域；資料將在整個帳戶生命週期內保留在所選區域。如果您有特定的合規性限制（例如，特定的成員國位置或主權雲端提供者），請在意向階段告知我們，我們會將其納入上線計畫。

 Architecture

 建築學

## A clustered service with high availability built in.｜具有高可用性的叢集服務。

 Each customer runs on a Stalwart cluster sized to its tier. There are no single points of failure inside a tenant; node loss is handled by the cluster without operator intervention.

 每個客戶都運行在與其層級相符的 Stalwart 叢集上。租戶內部不存在單點故障；節點遺失由叢集自動處理，無需維運人員幹預。

- Pluggable storage with replicated message and metadata stores.
具有複製訊息和元資料儲存的可插拔儲存。
- Sharded blob storage for attachments, calendar objects and files.
用於附件、日曆物件和文件的分片 Blob 儲存。
- Cluster coordination over peer-to-peer \(Zenoh\) or a managed message bus, with read replicas for hot paths.
透過點對點（Zenoh）或託管訊息匯流排進行叢集協調，並為熱路徑提供唯讀副本。
- ACME-managed TLS for mail, submission and HTTP listeners, with automatic renewal.
ACME管理TLS郵件、提交和HTTP監聽器，並自動續訂。
- DANE, MTA-STS and SMTP TLS reporting on outbound delivery.
DANE, MTA-STS和SMTP TLS報告出庫交貨狀況。
- Outbound MTA cluster role for queue scaling and delayed-delivery isolation.
出站MTA集群角色，用於隊列擴展和延遲交付隔離。

 Migration

 移民

## Move in without rebuilding.｜無需重建即可入住。

 Onboarding includes a migration engagement run by our team. Source systems we cover include Postfix and Dovecot, Cyrus, Zimbra, Microsoft Exchange \(and self-hosted Microsoft 365\), MDaemon, hMailServer and Courier; others on request. Mailboxes, folders, flags, calendars, address books and shared resources are carried over. We schedule a low-traffic cutover window per domain and run a co-existence period if you need both systems live at once. Full details, including timelines and tooling, are on the migration page.

 入職流程包括由我們團隊執行的遷移服務。我們支援的來源系統包括 Postfix 和 Dovecot、Cyrus、Zimbra、Microsoft Exchange（以及自架的 Microsoft 365）、MDaemon、hMailServer 和 Courier；其他系統可依需求提供。郵箱、資料夾、標記、行事曆、通訊錄和共用資源均會遷移。我們會為每個網域安排一個低流量的切換窗口，如果您需要兩個系統同時運行，我們會安排一段共存期。完整的詳細信息，包括時間表和工具，請訪問遷移頁面 。

 Support tiers

 支援層級

## Pick the level of operations involvement you need.｜選擇您需要的營運參與程度。

- **Standard.** Business-hours response on operational issues. Access to the customer portal, ticketing, and the same release notes and security advisories sent to Enterprise self-hosters.
**標準服務。** 在工作時間內回應營運問題。客戶可存取客戶入口網站、工單系統，並收到與企業自架用戶相同的版本說明和安全公告。
- **Enterprise standard.** Priority response, named technical contact, scheduled review calls, and signed SLAs covering availability and recovery objectives.
**企業標準。** 優先回應、指定技術聯絡人、定期審查電話會議以及涵蓋可用性和恢復目標的已簽署 SLA。
- **Enterprise on-demand.** Dedicated Support Engineer, custom SLAs \(including 24x7 with paged response\), lawful-interception assistance where applicable. Subject to the same 150-mailbox minimum used for self-hosted Premium Support.
**企業級按需服務。** 專屬支援工程師、客製化服務等級協定 \(SLA\)（包括 24x7 全天候尋呼回應）、以及在適用情況下提供合法攔截協助。最低郵箱數量要求與自託管高級支援服務相同，均為 150 個郵箱。
SLAs
 服務等級協定

## Contracted, not aspirational.｜已落實，而非抱負。

 Standard tiers include a service-credit-backed availability SLA on the mail and collaboration endpoints, plus targets for support response by severity. Enterprise tiers add per-incident recovery time and recovery point objectives negotiated at contract signing. Specific numeric targets are published with the commercial terms when the service launches.

 標準套餐包含郵件和協作端點的SLA服務信用支援可用性保障，以及以嚴重程度劃分的支援回應目標。企業套餐則增加了每次事件的恢復時間和恢復點目標，這些目標在合約簽訂時協商確定。具體的數值目標將在服務上線時隨商業條款一同公佈。

 Trust and compliance

 信任與合規

## Where your data lives and how it is protected.｜您的資料儲存在哪裡以及如何受到保護。

- **Data location.** Mail content, calendars, contacts and files stay in the region selected at sign-up. We publish the list of sub-processors and notify customers of changes in advance.
**資料儲存位置。**郵件內容、日曆、聯絡人和文件將保存在您註冊時選擇的區域。我們會公佈子處理方列表，並在變更前提前通知客戶。

- **Encryption at rest.** Storage volumes and blob backends are encrypted at rest. Per-mailbox S/MIME and OpenPGP encryption-at-rest is available for sensitive mailboxes.
**靜態資料加密。** 儲存磁碟區和 Blob 後端在靜態儲存時均進行加密。敏感信箱可使用 S/ MIME和 OpenPGP 靜態資料加密。
- **Encryption in transit.** TLS on every public listener; DANE, MTA-STS and TLS-RPT enforced on outbound. ACME-managed certificates with automatic renewal.
**傳輸中加密。** TLS 在每個公共監聽器上； DANE, MTA-STS 和 TLS-RPT 在出站時強制執行。 ACME 託管證書，具有自動續訂功能。
- **Independent security audit.** Stalwart has been independently audited; the report is published in the security audit blog post. The managed service runs the audited codebase.
**獨立安全審計。** Stalwart 已透過獨立審計；審計報告發佈在安全審計部落格文章中。託管服務運行的是經過審計的程式碼庫。
- **DPA and GDPR.** A Data Processing Addendum is available before contract signing. Stalwart Labs acts as processor for customer mailbox content; the sub-processor list is part of the legal pack.
**DPA和GDPR 。** 資料處理附錄可在合約簽署前取得。 Stalwart Labs 作為客戶郵箱內容的處理者；子處理者清單包含在法律文件中。
Pricing
 定價

## Per-mailbox, with hosting and operations bundled.｜按郵箱計費，包含託管和營運服務。

 Managed Email follows the per-mailbox bands published on the pricing page. Hosting, storage, bandwidth, support at the contracted tier and migration assistance are included in the per-mailbox figure. Optional add-ons \(custom SLAs, lawful interception, dedicated engineering time\) are quoted separately.

 託管電子郵件服務遵循定價頁面上公佈的郵箱價格區間。每個郵箱的價格包含託管、儲存、頻寬、合約層級的支援以及遷移協助。可選附加服務（自訂服務等級協定、合法攔截、專屬工程團隊時間）將單獨報價。
