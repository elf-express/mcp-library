 How licensing works

 授權機制是如何運作的

## Per-mailbox subscription, annual or monthly.｜按郵箱訂閱，可選擇按年或按月付費。

 Two billing cycles are available: **annual**and**monthly**, for organisations that prefer a shorter commitment. Only user and group mailboxes count toward the total. Mailing lists and aliases are not billed. Every plan includes unlimited domains, tenants, mailing lists and aliases at no extra cost.

 我們提供兩種計費週期：**年付**和**月付**，適合希望縮短合約期限的組織。計費僅包含使用者和群組郵箱，郵件清單和別名不計費。所有套餐均包含無限量的網域、租戶、郵件清單和別名，無需額外付費。

## How a license is issued.｜許可證是如何頒發的。

 After purchase at the [Stalwart Labs licensing portal](https://license.stalw.art/buy), the key is available immediately and activates Enterprise features as soon as it is installed on the server. A 30-day Enterprise trial is available at the [trial portal](https://license.stalw.art/trial), with full access to Enterprise features for evaluation.

 在 [Stalwart Labs 授權入口網站](https://license.stalw.art/buy)購買後，金鑰將立即生效，安裝到伺服器後即可啟動企業版功能。您也可以在 [試用入口網站](https://license.stalw.art/trial)取得 30 天的企業版試用版，試用期間可完全存取企業版功能進行評估。

**錨定到主機名稱域**，而不是單一伺服器。 `*.example.org` 的金鑰涵蓋該網域下無限數量的伺服器。

**無限客戶端網域名稱**：網域限制僅適用於伺服器主機名，不適用於使用者收發郵件的網域。

Premium Support

## Premium Support is included from 150 mailboxes.｜包含來自 150 個郵箱的高級支援。

 Enterprise subscriptions of 150 mailboxes or more include Premium Support: an initial response within 48 hours during business hours, expedited bug fixing and influence on the product roadmap. Smaller Enterprise subscriptions \(below 150 mailboxes\) get the Enterprise feature set only; for help and questions, those customers rely on the same community support channels as Community-edition users. On-demand options for Premium Support customers include a dedicated Support Engineer, a custom SLA with tighter response targets, and lawful interception. The coverage comparison and the list of on-demand services live on the Support page.

 企業版訂閱（150 個郵箱或以上）包含高級支援服務：工作時間內 48 小時內獲得初步回應、快速修復漏洞以及對產品路線圖的影響力。規模較小的企業版訂閱（150 個郵箱以下）僅提供企業版功能集；如需協助或有任何疑問，這些客戶可與社群版使用者一樣，使用相同的社群支援管道。高級支援客戶的按需服務包括專屬支援工程師、具有更嚴格回應目標的定製版SLA以及合法攔截。服務覆蓋範圍比較和按需服務清單可在 支援頁面上查看。

 Frequently asked

 常見問題

## Pricing FAQ.｜定價FAQ 。

- Can I purchase fewer than 25 mailboxes?
我可以購買少於 25 個郵箱嗎？
Not at the moment: 25 mailboxes is the smallest Enterprise subscription. If your organisation has fewer users, a 25-mailbox license still covers you fully, leaves room to grow, and can be extended with more mailboxes at any time. If you do not need Enterprise features, the Community edition is free, with no mailbox limit.
 目前沒有：25 個郵箱是企業版訂閱的最小數量。如果您的組織使用者較少，25 個郵箱的許可仍然完全滿足您的需求，並預留了擴展空間，並且可以隨時增加郵箱數量。如果您不需要企業版功能，社群版是免費的，沒有信箱數量限制。

- Can I move the license to a different server?
我可以將許可證轉移到另一台伺服器嗎？
Yes, as long as the server hostname falls under the licensed domain \(for example, anywhere under `*.example.org`\).
 是的，只要伺服器主機名稱屬於許可域（例如， `*.example.org`下的任何內容）。

- Do I need a separate license for each region or data centre?
我是否需要為每個地區或資料中心單獨購買許可證？
No. One key covers every server under the licensed hostname domain, regardless of where it runs.
 不。一個密鑰可以涵蓋授權主機名稱域下的所有伺服器，無論它們運行在何處。

- What happens if my mailbox count exceeds my plan mid-cycle?
如果我的郵箱數量在周期中途超過了套餐限制，會發生什麼情況？
Existing mailboxes keep working without restriction; the server simply stops creating new mailboxes once your licensed count is reached. To unblock new mailboxes, buy additional licenses at [license.stalw.art](https://license.stalw.art/) and apply them to the running server: there is no downtime and the system generates a prorated invoice for the remaining period.
 現有郵箱將繼續正常使用；伺服器會在達到許可郵箱數量上限後停止建立新郵箱。要解鎖新郵箱，請在 [license.stalw.art](https://license.stalw.art/)購買額外許可證並將其應用到正在運行的伺服器：此過程不會造成停機，系統會根據剩餘期限產生按比例計算的帳單。

- Can I purchase additional mailboxes mid-cycle?
我可以在服務週期中途購買額外的郵箱嗎？
Yes. Buy more mailbox licenses at [license.stalw.art](https://license.stalw.art/) at any time during your billing period, then apply the new key to the running server: it takes effect without a restart, and the system charges only for the prorated remainder of the cycle.
 是的。您可以在計費週期內的任何時間透過 [license.stalw.art](https://license.stalw.art/)購買更多郵箱許可證，然後將新金鑰應用到正在運行的伺服器：無需重新啟動即可生效，系統僅按比例收取剩餘週期的費用。

- Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
我可以切換按月付費和按年付費嗎？
Yes, in either direction. The change is applied at [license.stalw.art](https://license.stalw.art/) and takes effect without downtime; the system reconciles the change with a prorated invoice for the rest of the period.
 是的，雙向都可以。變更將在 [license.stalw.art](https://license.stalw.art/)處應用，無需停機即可生效；系統會將變更與剩餘期間的按比例計費的發票進行核對。

- What happens if I cancel my Enterprise subscription?
如果我取消企業版訂閱會怎樣？
The server reverts to the Community edition automatically. Enterprise-only features are disabled, but no data is lost and standard email and collaboration service continues without interruption.
 伺服器將自動恢復到社群版。企業版功能將被停用，但資料不會遺失，標準的電子郵件和協作服務將繼續不間斷地運作。
