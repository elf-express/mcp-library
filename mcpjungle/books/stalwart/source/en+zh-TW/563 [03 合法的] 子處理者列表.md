# Sub-processor list｜子處理者列表

> 章節：\\\[legal｜合法的\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-79&gt;\\\)

Stalwart Labs Ltd engages the following sub-processors to support the delivery of paid services. The list is provided for transparency and is updated when sub-processors are added, removed, or replaced.

 Stalwart Labs Ltd 委託以下分包商提供付費服務。此清單旨在提高透明度，並在分包商新增、移除或更換時更新。

## Services operated in-house \(no sub-processor\)｜服務均由公司內部營運（無分包商）

 The following Stalwart Labs Ltd services are operated in-house from infrastructure in the United Kingdom and do not currently rely on a third-party sub-processor for the underlying processing:

 以下 Stalwart Labs Ltd 服務均由公司在英國的基礎設施內部經營，目前不依賴第三方子處理商進行底層處理：

- the marketing site, documentation, and blog at **stalw.art** \(served via a content delivery network for static delivery, no personal data of visitors is processed beyond standard server logs\);
行銷網站、文件和部落格位於 **stalw.art**（透過內容分發網路提供靜態內容，除標準伺服器日誌外，不處理訪客的任何個人資料）；
- the Licensing Portal at **license.stalw.art** \(account, subscription, and invoice management\);
**license.stalw.art** 上的授權入口網站（帳戶、訂閱和發票管理）；
- the Support Portal at **support.stalw.art** \(community forum and private priority-support tickets\).
支援入口網站為 **support.stalw.art**（社區論壇和私人優先支援工單）。
The live, authoritative version of this list is published on this page and is updated whenever sub-processors change. Enterprise and Managed Email customers may request notification of material changes as part of their DPA.
 本列表的即時權威版本發佈在本頁面上，並在子處理器發生變更時更新。企業和託管電子郵件客戶可以要求在其 DPA中接收重大變更通知。
