# Administrators｜管理員

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[auth｜認證\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-3&gt;\\\) › \\\[authorization｜授權\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-5&gt;\\\)

Stalwart has no dedicated “administrator account” type in the directory. Administrative capabilities are granted by assigning specific permissions to regular individual accounts. This avoids handing out blanket administrative privileges and allows each administrator to hold only the permissions required for the tasks they perform.

 Stalwart 目錄中沒有專門的「管理員帳戶」類型。管理權限是透過向普通的個人帳戶分配特定的權限\([https://stalw.art/docs/auth/principals/individual](https://stalw.art/docs/auth/principals/individual)\) \([https://stalw.art/docs/auth/authorization/permissions](https://stalw.art/docs/auth/authorization/permissions)\) ）來授予的。這樣可以避免授予過多的管理權限，並確保每個管理員只擁有執行其任務所需的權限。

 Assigning permissions to ordinary accounts lets administrative responsibilities be distributed without compromising security. For example, one account may be granted the ability to manage domain settings and user accounts without being able to reconfigure system-wide services. This principle of least privilege reduces the risk of accidental or malicious changes to critical parts of the server.

 為普通帳戶分配權限可以在不損害安全性的前提下分散管理職責。例如，可以授予某個帳戶管理網域設定和使用者帳戶的權限，但不允許其重新配置係統級服務。這種最小權限原則降低了伺服器關鍵部分被意外或惡意更改的風險。

 Administrators can create one or more Role objects that group administrative permissions together and assign those roles to the appropriate accounts. This provides consistent control over who can perform which administrative tasks.

 管理員可以建立一個或多個 角色對象，將管理權限分組，並將這些角色指派給相應的帳戶。這樣可以對哪些使用者可以執行哪些管理任務進行統一控制。

 For emergency access when the regular administrative accounts are unavailable, see the recovery administrator mechanism. It is an environment-variable credential that is only honoured while the server is running in recovery mode or during first-time bootstrap, and is intended to be set temporarily while an incident is resolved.

 當常規管理帳戶不可用時，如需緊急訪問，請參閱恢復管理員機制。這是一個環境變數憑證，僅在伺服器處於復原模式或首次引導期間有效，旨在事件解決期間臨時設定。

## Impersonation｜冒充

 There is no dedicated master-user configuration. Mailbox-wide access is instead granted by assigning the `impersonate` permission to a regular account, typically through a Role. Any account that holds this permission can sign in on behalf of another account by supplying a composite login of the form `<target>%<impersonator>` together with the impersonator’s password.

 沒有專門的主用戶配置。郵箱範圍的存取權限是透過將`impersonate` 權限分配給普通帳戶來授予的，通常是透過 角色來實現。任何擁有此權限的帳戶都可以透過使用`<target>%<impersonator>`形式的複合登入資訊以及模擬器的密碼來代表其他帳戶登入。

 For example, when the impersonating account is `[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)` and the target mailbox belongs to `[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)`, signing in as `[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)%[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)` with the password of `[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)` grants access to `john`’s mailbox. The same convention works for single-label account names on deployments that do not use `user@domain` logins. Because this access is broad, the `impersonate` permission should be granted sparingly and only for legitimate administrative tasks such as maintenance or incident response.

 例如，當模擬帳號為`[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)` ，目標信箱屬於`[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)`時，使用`[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)`的密碼以`[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)%[[email protected]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)`身分登入即可存取`john`的信箱。對於不使用`user@domain`登入的部署，單一標籤帳戶名稱也適用相同的約定。由於此存取權限範圍廣泛， `impersonate`權限應謹慎授予，且僅用於維護或事件回應等合法的管理任務。
