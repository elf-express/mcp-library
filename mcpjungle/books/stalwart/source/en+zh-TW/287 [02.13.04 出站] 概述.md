# Overview｜概述

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[mta｜捷運\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-47&gt;\\\) › \\\[outbound｜出站\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-52&gt;\\\)

Stalwart MTA provides a flexible framework for managing outbound email delivery. Messages sent to both local and external domains are processed through a strategy-driven architecture that allows precise control over how, when, and where each message is delivered. Delivery strategies are evaluated dynamically for each recipient, adapting behaviour to the message context, such as the recipient domain, the sender’s identity, message type, or assigned priority.

 Stalwart MTA提供了一個靈活的框架來管理外發郵件。傳送到本地和外部網域的郵件都透過 策略驅動的架構進行處理，從而可以精確控制每封郵件的投遞方式、時間和地點。投遞策略會針對每個收件者動態評估，並根據郵件上下文（例如收件者網域、寄件者身分、郵件類型或指派的優先順序）調整其行為。

 Through its delivery strategies, Stalwart controls every aspect of outbound delivery, including queue selection, retry policies, routing logic, connection parameters, and transport-security enforcement. These strategies work together to ensure messages are handled according to defined policies while allowing per-recipient delivery decisions, independent retry logic, and conditional routing.

 Stalwart 透過其交付策略，控制出站交付的各個方面，包括佇列選擇、重試策略、路由邏輯、連接參數和傳輸安全機制。這些策略協同工作，確保訊息按照預先定義的策略進行處理，同時允許針對每個收件人做出交付決策、採用獨立的重試邏輯和條件路由。

 Outbound delivery supports isolated virtual queues for different traffic types, secure transmission with DANE and MTA-STS, configurable STARTTLS behaviour, and the ability to relay mail through designated hosts when necessary. The system is designed to scale, manage delivery concurrency, and enforce compliance with authentication and security standards.

 出站郵件投遞支援針對不同流量類型的獨立虛擬佇列，採用DANE和MTA-STS進行安全傳輸，支援可設定的STARTTLS行為，並可在必要時透過指定主機轉送郵件。該系統旨在實現可擴展性，管理投遞並發，並確保符合身份驗證和安全標準。

 The following sections describe how to configure outbound delivery, beginning with the definition of strategies and virtual queues.

 以下各節將介紹如何設定出站交付，首先定義策略和虛擬佇列。
