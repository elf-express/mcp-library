# IMAP

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[migration｜遷移\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-37&gt;\\\) › \\\[proxy｜代理人\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-42&gt;\\\) › \\\[routing｜路由\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-46&gt;\\\)

The IMAP listener accepts client connections, conducts the pre-authentication dialogue, and bridges the authenticated session to the resolved backend. It serves the implicit-TLS convention on port 993 and the STARTTLS convention on port 143, depending on the listener’s `tls` mode.

 IMAP監聽器接受客戶端連接，執行預先認證對話，並將已認證的會話橋接到已解析的後端。它根據監聽器的`tls`模式，在連接埠 993 上提供隱式TLS約定，在連接埠 143 上提供STARTTLS約定。

## Pre-authentication｜預認證

 On connection the proxy sends an untagged `OK` greeting that embeds the advertised capability list and the configured banner. Before authentication it answers a small set of commands itself: `CAPABILITY` returns the advertised capabilities, `NOOP` and `ID` are acknowledged, `LOGOUT` ends the connection, and `STARTTLS` upgrades the connection on a STARTTLS listener. All other commands are refused as not allowed in the pre-authentication state.

 連線建立後，代理會傳送未標記的`OK`問候語，其中包含已通告的功能清單和已設定的橫幅。在驗證之前，代理程式會自行回應一組簡短的指令： `CAPABILITY`回傳已通告的功能， `NOOP`和`ID`用於確認， `LOGOUT`用於結束連接， `STARTTLS`用於在STARTTLS監聽器上升級連接。所有其他命令在身份驗證前狀態下均被拒絕，因為它們是不允許的。

 The advertised capabilities are drawn from `[capabilities.imap]`. The `advertise` list provides the base tokens \(by default including `IMAP4rev2`, `IMAP4rev1`, `SASL-IR`, `LITERAL+` and others\), to which the proxy appends the available `AUTH=` mechanisms. When cleartext authentication is not permitted on the current connection, the proxy advertises `LOGINDISABLED` and adds `STARTTLS` on a STARTTLS listener, signalling to clients that the connection must be secured first.

 所發表的功能源自於`[capabilities.imap]`列表提供基礎令牌`advertise`預設包含`IMAP4rev2`, `IMAP4rev1`, `SASL-IR`, `LITERAL+`等），代理會將可用的`AUTH=`機制加入這些基礎令牌中。當目前連線不允許明文認證時，代理程式會公佈`LOGINDISABLED` ，並在STARTTLS監聽器上加入`STARTTLS` ，以此向客戶端發出訊號，表示連線必須先進行安全驗證。

## Authentication｜驗證

 Two authentication paths are accepted. The `AUTHENTICATE` command carries a SASL mechanism, one of `PLAIN`, `LOGIN`, `OAUTHBEARER` or `XOAUTH2`; the initial response may be supplied inline \(when the client uses `SASL-IR`\) or provided after the proxy’s continuation prompt. The `LOGIN` command supplies a username and password directly, which the proxy treats as `PLAIN`.

 接受兩種身份驗證路徑。 `AUTHENTICATE`指令攜帶SASL機制，`PLAIN`, `LOGIN`, `OAUTHBEARER`或`XOAUTH2`之一；初始回應可以內聯提供（當客戶端使用`SASL-IR`時）或在代理的繼續提示之後提供。 `LOGIN` 指令直接提供使用者名稱和密碼，代理將其視為`PLAIN`。

`AUTHENTICATE LOGIN` is advertised as `AUTH=LOGIN` whenever `LOGIN` appears in the `sasl` list and cleartext authentication is permitted on the connection. The proxy runs the two-prompt exchange itself, sending the base64 `Username:` and `Password:` challenges and accepting the client’s responses, then reconstructs the result as a `PLAIN` challenge; the backend leg therefore only ever sees `PLAIN`. A client may also supply the base64 username as the initial response, and may abort the exchange with `*`.

 當`LOGIN`出現在`sasl`清單中，且連線允許明文認證時， `AUTHENTICATE LOGIN`會被通告為`AUTH=LOGIN` 。代理伺服器自行執行雙提示交換，發送 base64 編碼的`Username:`和`Password:`質詢，並接受客戶端的回應，然後將結果重構為`PLAIN`質詢；因此，後端只會看到`PLAIN` 。客戶端也可以將 base64 編碼的使用者名稱作為初始回應，並且可以使用`*`中止交換。

 Cleartext authentication, meaning `LOGIN`, `AUTHENTICATE PLAIN` or `AUTHENTICATE LOGIN`, is gated on transport security: unless `allow_plain_auth_without_tls` is set, it is refused with a privacy-required error until the connection is encrypted by implicit TLS or STARTTLS. From the accepted credentials the proxy extracts the routing identifier as described under Routing, resolves the destination, and proceeds to the backend.

 明文認證（即`LOGIN`, `AUTHENTICATE PLAIN`或`AUTHENTICATE LOGIN` ）受傳輸層安全機制的限制：除非設定了`allow_plain_auth_without_tls` ，否則連接將被拒絕並返回隱私要求錯誤，直到連接通過隱式TLS或STARTTLS加密。代理伺服器會從已接受的憑證中提取路由標識符（如路由中所述），解析目標位址，然後繼續存取後端。

## Backend handshake｜後端握手

 The proxy connects to the destination’s IMAP endpoint and reads the backend’s greeting. On a STARTTLS endpoint it confirms the backend advertises `STARTTLS`, issues the command, and upgrades the connection before continuing. It then verifies the backend leg is encrypted \(or that the destination has opted into plaintext authentication\).

 代理連接到目標端的IMAP端點並讀取後端的問候語。在STARTTLS端點上，它確認後端已發布`STARTTLS` ，發出命令，並在繼續之前升級連接。然後，它會驗證後端連結是否已加密（或目標端是否已選擇明文驗證）。

 When the destination uses `XCLIENT` forwarding, the proxy sends an `ID` command before authenticating, provided the backend advertised `ID` support, conveying the originating and connected addresses and ports, the forwarding time-to-live, a session identifier and the transport security in the `x-originating-ip`, `x-connected-ip` and related fields. It then replays the client’s `AUTHENTICATE` command to the backend. The initial response is sent inline on the command line only when the backend advertised `SASL-IR` in the capability list read from its greeting \(or, on a STARTTLS endpoint, from its response to `STARTTLS`\); otherwise the command is sent on its own and the base64 response follows the backend’s continuation prompt, as required by servers that do not implement `SASL-IR`. The proxy relays the result to the client and on success bridges the two connections. From that point the client and backend exchange IMAP traffic directly, including long-lived `IDLE` sessions, until the connection closes or the `bridge_idle` timeout is reached.

 當目標使用`XCLIENT`轉送時，代理會在進行身份驗證之前發送`ID`命令（前提是後端已聲明支援`ID`轉送），該命令會傳遞來源位址和連接位址及連接埠、轉送生存時間、會話識別碼以及`x-originating-ip`, `x-connected-ip`及相關欄位中的傳輸安全資訊。然後，代理會將客戶端的`AUTHENTICATE`指令重播給後端。僅當後端在其問候語（或，在STARTTLS端點上，在其對`STARTTLS`的響​​​應）中聲明了`SASL-IR`轉發功能時，初始響應才會以`SASL-IR`行內聯方式發送；否則代理伺服器將結果轉送給客戶端，並在成功後建立兩個連線。從那時起，客戶端和後端直接交換IMAP流量，包括長時間的`IDLE`會話，直到連線關閉或達到`bridge_idle`超時時間。
