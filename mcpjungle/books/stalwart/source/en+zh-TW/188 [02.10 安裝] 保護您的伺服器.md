# Securing your server｜保護您的伺服器

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[install｜安裝\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-29&gt;\\\)

Stalwart is secure by design. From the moment it’s deployed, it applies safe, security-conscious defaults that follow best practices for modern mail server operation. Out of the box, it includes built-in defense mechanisms to protect against common threats. For example, Stalwart can automatically detect and block IP addresses that attempt brute-force password attacks, guess account names, scan for vulnerabilities, or launch SYN flood attacks. These protections operate continuously to reduce the risk of abuse and compromise.

 Stalwart 從設計之初就注重安全性。從部署之日起，它便應用安全可靠的預設設定\([https://stalw.art/docs/configuration/#safe-defaults](https://stalw.art/docs/configuration/#safe-defaults)\) ），遵循現代郵件伺服器運行的最佳實踐。它開箱即用，內建多種防禦機制，可抵禦常見威脅（ \([https://stalw.art/docs/server/auto-ban](https://stalw.art/docs/server/auto-ban)\) ）。例如，Stalwart 可以自動偵測並阻止嘗試暴力破解密碼、猜測帳號名稱、掃描漏洞或發動洪水攻擊SYN IP位址。這些保護措施持續運行，以降低濫用和入侵的風險。

 While Stalwart provides a strong security foundation, server administrators still play a critical role in maintaining a secure environment. This section offers additional recommendations and best practices to further harden your server, minimize attack surfaces, and ensure safe operation in a variety of deployment scenarios, from small personal setups to large, internet-facing infrastructure.

 雖然 Stalwart 提供了強大的安全基礎，但伺服器管理員在維護安全環境中仍然扮演著至關重要的角色。本節提供了一些額外的建議和最佳實踐，旨在進一步強化您的伺服器，最大限度地減少攻擊面，並確保在各種部署場景（從小型個人環境到大型面向互聯網的基礎設施）下安全運行。

## Disable unused ports｜禁用未使用的連接埠

 To simplify initial setup, Stalwart enables network listeners for all supported services by default, including IMAP, HTTP, SMTP, POP3, and ManageSieve. While this ensures that the server is immediately functional in most environments, it is strongly recommended that administrators **disable any services and their associated ports that are not actively in use**. Doing so improves security, reduces the server’s attack surface, and minimizes resource usage.

 為了簡化初始設置，Stalwart 預設啟用所有支援服務（包括IMAP, HTTP, SMTP, POP3和 ManageSieve）的網路監聽器。雖然這可以確保伺服器在大多數環境中立即正常運行，但強烈建議管理員**停用任何未使用的服務及其關聯連接埠**。這樣做可以提高安全性，縮小伺服器的攻擊面，並最大限度地減少資源佔用。

 Unused ports represent potential entry points for attackers. Even if the underlying service is secure, each open port can still be subject to brute-force attempts, protocol-specific exploits, or resource exhaustion attacks. Therefore, as a best practice, only enable the ports and services you actually require.

 未使用的連接埠是攻擊者的潛在入口點。即使底層服務是安全的，每個開放的連接埠仍然可能遭受暴力破解、協定特定漏洞或資源耗盡攻擊。因此，最佳實踐是僅啟用您實際需要的連接埠和服務。

 Administrators can disable unused ports in one of two ways:

 管理員可以透過以下兩種方式之一停用未使用的連接埠：

- **Remove the corresponding listener from the Stalwart configuration**. This ensures that the service no longer binds to the port.
**從 Stalwart 配置中移除對應的 listener**。這將確保該服務不再綁定到該連接埠。
- **Block incoming traffic at the network or host firewall level**, which prevents external access even if the listener is active internally.
**在網路或主機防火牆層級封鎖傳入流量**，即使監聽器在內部處於作用中狀態，也能阻止外部存取。
Stalwart uses a variety of ports to support email delivery, access, and administrative tasks. Knowing what each port does will help you decide which ones to keep open and which can be safely closed.
 Stalwart 使用多種連接埠來支援電子郵件傳送、存取和管理任務。了解每個連接埠的功能將有助於您決定哪些連接埠需要保持開放，哪些連接埠可以安全地關閉。

 The essential ports are:

 基本連接埠包括：

- **Port 25 \(SMTP\)**: Used for receiving email from other mail servers. This port must remain open for your domain to receive external email traffic reliably.
**連接埠 25 \( SMTP \)**：用於接收來自其他郵件伺服器的電子郵件。此連接埠必須保持開放，您的網域才能可靠地接收外部電子郵件流量。
- **Port 465 \(SMTPS\)**: Handles email submission with implicit TLS encryption. Recommended for securely sending outgoing mail from user clients. It is best to keep this port open and use it in preference to port 587.
**連接埠 465 \( SMTPS \)**：處理使用隱含加密TLS的電子郵件提交。建議用戶用戶端使用此連接埠安全地發送外發郵件。最好保持此端口開放，並優先使用此端口，而不是端口 587。
- **Port 993 \(IMAPS\)**: IMAP over implicit TLS. Required for secure email access via IMAP clients. Keep this port open to allow encrypted mail retrieval.
**連接埠 993 \( IMAPS \)**： IMAP透過隱式TLS進行加密。透過IMAP客戶端安全存取電子郵件需要此連接埠。請保持此連接埠開放以允許加密郵件的檢索。
- **Port 443 \(HTTPS\)**: A critical port used for multiple secure web-based services, including web administration, JMAP, OAuth, TLS certificate provisioning \(ACME challenges\), autoconfig, and MTA-STS. This port must remain open in nearly all deployments.
**連接埠 443（HTTPS\)**: 用於多個安全 Web 服務的關鍵端口，包括 Web 管理, JMAPOAuth, TLS 證書配置（ACME 挑戰\), 自動配置， 和 MTA-STS在幾乎所有部署環境中，此連接埠都必須保持開放狀態。
The non-essential ports are:
 非必要端口包括：

- **Port 587 \(SMTP Submission\)**: Handles email submission using STARTTLS. If all your clients are configured to use port 465 with implicit TLS, port 587 can be disabled for simplicity and security.
**連接埠 587（ SMTP提交）**：處理使用STARTTLS電子郵件提交。如果所有用戶端都配置為使用連接埠 465 並隱式啟用TLS ，則可以為了簡化操作和提高安全性而停用連接埠 587。
- **Port 143 \(IMAP4\)**: The standard IMAP port without encryption. This port should generally be disabled in favor of the encrypted IMAPS port \(993\).
**連接埠 143 \( IMAP4 \)**：標準的IMAP端口，未加密。通常應停用此端口，而使用加密的IMAPS端口 \(993\)。
- **Port 4190 \(ManageSieve\)**: Used to manage Sieve scripts remotely. Only keep this port open if your users actively use Sieve for email filtering rules.
**連接埠 4190（ManageSieve）**：用於遠端管理 Sieve 腳本。只有當您的使用者經常使用 Sieve 進行郵件過濾規則時，才需要保持此連接埠開放。
- **Port 110 \(POP3\)**and**Port 995 \(POP3S\)**: These are used for retrieving email via POP3. Unless you have a legacy requirement or specific use case, POP3 is largely obsolete in favor of IMAP and can usually be disabled.
**連接埠 110 \( POP3 \)**和**連接埠 995 \( POP3S \)**：這些連接埠用於透過POP3收發電子郵件。除非您有遺留系統需求或特定用例，否則POP3已被IMAP取代，通常可以停用。

- **Port 8080 \(HTTP\)**: Provided mainly for initial setup. It is strongly recommended to disable this port after setup is complete to prevent unauthenticated, unencrypted access.
**連接埠 8080 \( HTTP \)**：主要用於初始設定。強烈建議在設定完成後停用此端口，以防止未經身份驗證和未加密的存取。
By carefully reviewing and disabling any unused network ports, you can significantly strengthen your server’s security posture. Whether you’re running a small private mail server or a production system, this step is essential for reducing exposure to potential attacks. Use either Stalwart’s configuration system or your server’s firewall to enforce these changes consistently.
 透過仔細檢查並停用所有未使用的網路端口，您可以顯著增強伺服器的安全防護能力。無論您執行的是小型私有郵件伺服器還是生產系統，此步驟對於降低潛在攻擊風險至關重要。您可以使用 Stalwart 的設定係統或伺服器防火牆來持續強制執行這些變更。

## Enforce HTTP Access Control｜強制執行HTTP存取控制

 Stalwart’s HTTP listener plays a central role in enabling many of the server’s modern capabilities. It is used to provide access to a range of services, including JMAP, WebDAV, management APIs, ACME certificate issuance, autoconfig/autodiscover protocols, `.well-known` resources, metrics endpoints, and OAuth-based authentication.

 Stalwart 的HTTP監聽器在實現伺服器的許多現代功能方面發揮核心作用。它用於提供對一系列服務的訪問，包括JMAP 、WebDAV、管理 API、 ACME證書頒發、自動配置/自動發現協議、 `.well-known`資源、指標端點和基於 OAuth 的身份驗證。

 This broad functionality also means that the HTTP listener exposes multiple endpoints by default. In scenarios where you want to allow access to only a subset of these features \(such as enabling JMAP but disabling WebDAV or public metrics access\), you will need to define HTTP Access Control rules.

 這種廣泛的功能也意味著HTTP監聽器預設公開多個端點。如果您只想允許存取這些功能的子集（例如啟用JMAP但停用 WebDAV 或公共指標存取），則需要定義 HTTP存取控制規則 。

 HTTP Access Control rules are logical expressions evaluated for every incoming HTTP request. These rules determine whether access to a particular URL should be granted, based on criteria such as the request path, method, source IP, authentication status, or other conditions. This provides fine-grained control over which HTTP endpoints are accessible and under what circumstances.

 HTTP存取控制規則是針對每個傳入的HTTP請求進行評估的邏輯表達式。這些規則根據請求路徑、方法、來源IP 、身份驗證狀態或其他條件等標準，決定是否應授予對特定URL的存取權限。這可以對哪些HTTP端點可存取以及在何種情況下可存取進行精細控制。

 To tighten security and reduce unnecessary exposure, it is highly recommended to review the list of HTTP paths used by Stalwart and explicitly disable access to any endpoints that are not needed in your deployment. This is especially important for internet-facing servers, where unused or misconfigured services could be targeted.

 為了加強安全性並減少不必要的風險暴露，強烈建議您檢查 Stalwart 使用的路徑清單\([https://stalw.art/docs/http/](https://stalw.art/docs/http/)\) HTTP ），並明確停用部署中不需要的任何端點的存取。這對於面向互聯網的伺服器尤其重要，因為未使用的或配置錯誤的服務可能成為攻擊目標。

 You can implement access restrictions by creating appropriate HTTP Access Control rules in your configuration. These rules allow you to enforce access policies tailored to your use case, ensuring that only the necessary HTTP-based services are available.

 您可以透過在配置中建立適當的 HTTP存取控制規則來實施存取限制。這些規則允許您強制執行針對您的用例量身定制的存取策略，確保只有必要的基於HTTP的服務可用。

 Taking the time to configure HTTP Access Control not only improves your server’s security but also enhances its performance and predictability by eliminating unwanted or unused endpoints.

 花時間配置HTTP存取控制不僅可以提高伺服器的安全性，還可以透過消除不需要或未使用的端點來提高其效能和可預測性。

## Understand administrator roles｜了解管理員角色

 The first time Stalwart is started without a `config.json`, the server enters bootstrap mode and prints a one-time randomly generated administrator password to the console. That credential is used solely to complete the initial setup through the web-based setup wizard. Once the wizard finishes and the server provisions its permanent administrator account, the temporary bootstrap credential no longer applies. The password is shown only once on start-up, so it must be captured from the console before the logs scroll away.

 首次啟動 Stalwart 時，如果未指定`config.json` ，伺服器將進入 引導模式 ，並在控制台列印一個隨機產生的一次性管理員密碼。此憑證僅用於透過基於 Web 的設定精靈完成初始設定。精靈完成後，伺服器配置其永久管理員帳戶，臨時引導憑證將不再適用。密碼僅在啟動時顯示一次，因此必須在日誌滾動消失之前從控制台中取得。

 For emergencies, such as a misconfigured external directory that blocks every regular administrator from signing in, Stalwart provides the recovery administrator. The credential is supplied through the `STALWART_RECOVERY_ADMIN` environment variable in the form `username:password`, and is only honoured while the server is running in recovery mode or during bootstrap. It is intended as a temporary rescue mechanism, not a permanent login, and should be removed from the environment once the incident that required it has been resolved.

 對於緊急情況，例如外部目錄配置錯誤導致所有常規管理員無法登錄，Stalwart 提供了 恢復管理員 。憑證透過`STALWART_RECOVERY_ADMIN`環境變數以`username:password`形式提供，並且僅在伺服器以 恢復模式運行或啟動期間有效。它旨在作為一種臨時救援機制，而非永久登入方式，一旦導致問題的事件得到解決，就應將其從環境中移除。

 For day-to-day operations, it is best practice to define **multiple administrator accounts with specific, limited permissions**. For instance, one admin might be responsible only for managing user accounts, another for modifying server configurations, and another for overseeing the SMTP queue. Although dividing responsibilities in this way might seem inconvenient, it significantly reduces the potential impact of compromised credentials or accidental misconfiguration by limiting access to only the necessary functions.

 對於日常操作，最佳實踐是定義**多個具有特定的、有限的權限**的管理員帳戶。例如，一名管理員可能只負責管理使用者帳戶，另一位管理員負責修改伺服器配置，另一位管理員負責監督SMTP佇列。儘管以這種方式劃分職責可能看起來不方便，但它透過限制僅存取必要的功能，顯著減少了憑證洩漏或意外錯誤配置的潛在影響。

 In addition to role separation, it is vital to enforce a key security rule: **administrator accounts should never be used to access IMAP, JMAP, or WebDAV services**. These services are intended for regular user access and should be isolated from administrative functions to prevent misuse or exposure of elevated credentials through client software.

 除了角色分離之外，還必須強制執行一條關鍵的安全性規則：**管理員帳號絕不能用於存取IMAP, JMAP或WebDAV服務**。這些服務僅供普通用戶訪問，應與管理功能隔離，以防止濫用或透過客戶端軟體洩漏提升的憑證。

 By defining clear administrative roles and restricting unnecessary privileges, you enhance both the **security and maintainability** of your mail server environment.

 透過明確管理角色並限制不必要的權限，您可以提高郵件伺服器環境的**安全性和可維護性**。
