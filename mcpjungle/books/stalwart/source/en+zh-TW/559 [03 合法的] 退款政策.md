# Refund Policy｜退款政策

> 章節：\\\[legal｜合法的\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-79&gt;\\\)

Thank you for interest in purchasing a Stalwart subscription.

 感謝您有興趣購買 Stalwart 訂閱。

 If, for any reason, you are not completely satisfied with a purchase, we invite you to review our policy on refunds and returns. The following terms apply to any products you purchased with us.

 如果您出於任何原因對購買的產品不完全滿意，我們邀請您查看我們的退款和退貨政策。以下條款適用於您向我們購買的任何產品。

## Definitions｜定義

- **Company** \(‘the Company’, ‘we’, ‘us’, ‘our’\) refers to Stalwart Labs Ltd, 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX, UK.
**公司**（「本公司」、「我們」、「我們的」）指 Stalwart Labs Ltd，地址：128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX、UK。
- **Products** refer to the items offered for sale on the Service, such as the Stalwart Enterprise License subscription.
**產品**是指在服務上出售的物品，例如 Stalwart Enterprise 授權訂閱。

- **Orders** mean a request by you to purchase Products from us.
**訂單**是指您向我們購買產品的請求。

- **Service** refers to the Website.
**服務**指的是網站。

- **Website** refers to Stalwart Labs, accessible from [https://stalw.art](https://stalw.art).
**網站**指的是 Stalwart Labs，可從 [https://stalw.art](https://stalw.art) 存取。

- **You** means the individual accessing or using the Service, or the company or other legal entity on behalf of which such individual is accessing or using the Service.
**您** 指存取或使用本服務的個人，或代表該個人存取或使用本服務的公司或其他法律實體。
## Your order cancellation rights｜您的訂單取消權

 You are entitled to cancel your Order within 30 days without giving any reason for doing so. The deadline for cancelling an Order is 30 days from the date on which you received the Products.

 您有權在 30 天內取消訂單，無需給出任何理由。取消訂單的截止日期為您收到產品之日起 30 天。

 In order to exercise your right of cancellation, you must inform us of your decision by means of a clear statement, by email to **[\\[email protected\\]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#a0c8c5cccccfe0d3d4c1ccd78ec1d2d4)**.

 為了行使您的取消權，您必須透過電子郵件至 **[\\[email protected\\]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#a0c8c5cccccfe0d3d4c1ccd78ec1d2d4)**，以明確的聲明方式告知我們您的決定。

 We will reimburse you no later than 14 days from the day on which we receive the refund request. We will use the same means of payment as you used for the Order, and you will not incur any fees for such reimbursement.

 我們將在收到退款請求之日起 14 天內向您退款。我們將使用與您用於訂單的相同付款方式，並且您不會因此類報銷而產生任何費用。

## Contact us｜聯絡我們

 If you have any questions about our Returns and Refunds Policy, please contact us by email at **[\\[email protected\\]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#afc7cac3c3c0efdcdbcec3d881cedddb)**.

 如果您對我們的退貨和退款政策有任何疑問，請透過電子郵件與我們聯絡：**[\\[email protected\\]](https://stalw.art/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#afc7cac3c3c0efdcdbcec3d881cedddb)**。
