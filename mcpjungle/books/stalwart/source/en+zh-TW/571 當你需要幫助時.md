# Help when you｜當你需要幫助時

 Support

 支援

## Help when you need it.｜需要幫助時，我會伸出援手。

 All Stalwart support runs through a single portal at [support.stalw.art](https://support.stalw.art/), where any user can file a bug, suggest a new feature or get help from the community. Enterprise customers with Premium Support also have access to a private area on the same portal for opening tickets directly with Stalwart Labs.

 所有 Stalwart 支援服務均透過 [support.stalw.art](https://support.stalw.art/)上的單一入口網站進行，任何使用者都可以在此提交錯誤報告、提出新功能建議或獲得社群協助。擁有高級支援的企業客戶還可以存取同一入口網站上的一個專屬區域，直接向 Stalwart Labs 提交工單。

[Open the support portal](https://support.stalw.art/)

[開啟支援入口網站](https://support.stalw.art/)

 Community Support

 社區支持

## Free support for Community-edition users.｜為社群版用戶提供免費支援。

 Anyone running the Community edition can sign up for the support portal at no cost and post questions there. It is the supported channel for installation questions, configuration help, bug reports against the open-source release, feature discussion and general operator questions. Stalwart Labs engineers and experienced operators read the portal daily. Stalwart also has a presence on Discord, Reddit and Matrix. Those venues are for general discussion only; they are not support channels and questions posted there may not be answered.

 所有使用社群版的使用者都可以免費註冊支援入口網站並發布問題。該網站是發布安裝問題、配置幫助、開源版本錯誤報告、功能討論和一般運維問題的官方管道。 Stalwart Labs 的工程師和經驗豐富的維運人員每天都會瀏覽該入口網站。 Stalwart 在 Discord、Reddit 和 Matrix 上也設有官方帳號。這些平台僅用於一般討論，並非官方支援管道，發佈在那裡的問題可能無法得到解答。

[Open the support portal](https://support.stalw.art/)

[開啟支援入口網站](https://support.stalw.art/)

 Premium Support

 高級支援

## Priority Support for Enterprise customers, from 150 mailboxes.｜為企業客戶提供優先支持，來自 150 個郵箱。

 Premium Support is included at no extra cost with every Enterprise subscription of 150 mailboxes or more. Once activated, customers have access to a private area where tickets are opened directly with Stalwart Labs. Every ticket gets an initial response within 48 hours of receipt during business hours, and carries an SLA counter, so the response window against the contracted terms is visible to both sides at all times.

 所有擁有 150 個郵箱或以上數量的企業版訂閱均包含高級支援服務，無需額外付費。啟動後，客戶即可存取專屬區域，直接向 Stalwart Labs 提交工單。每張工單都會在工作時間內於收到後的 48 小時內獲得初步回复，並帶有SLA倒數計時，以便雙方隨時了解在合約條款下的回复時間。

- Initial response within 48 hours during business hours, with severity levels and a per-ticket SLA counter.
在工作時間內 48 小時內做出初步回應，並根據嚴重程度和每個工單的SLA計數器進行處理。
- Direct escalation path to the engineers who write Stalwart.
直接聯絡 Stalwart 的工程師，解決您的問題。
- Access to security advisories ahead of public disclosure where relevant.
在相關情況下，安全公告可在公開揭露前取得。
- Optional add-ons \(purchased separately\) include a dedicated Support Engineer, a custom SLA and lawful-interception assistance.
可選附加組件（單獨購買）包括專門的支援工程師、定制的SLA和合法攔截協助。
What's covered
 涵蓋內容

## Community Support and Premium Support, side by side.｜社區支持和高級支援並肩提供。

 Premium Support is included with Enterprise subscriptions of 150 mailboxes or more. The 48-hour initial response is the standard commitment in the licence agreement; tighter targets, including 24x7 paged response, are available as a custom SLA. Exact terms are confirmed at contract signing.

 企業版訂閱（150 個信箱或以上）包含高級支援服務。授權協議中的標準承諾是 48 小時內做出初步回應；如需更快速的回應，例如 24 小時全天候尋呼回應，可作為客製化服務SLA提供。具體條款將在合約簽訂時確認。
