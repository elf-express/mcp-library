# Management｜管理

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[mta｜捷運\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-47&gt;\\\)

The outbound message queue and the inbound and outbound report archives are runtime management objects, not configuration. They are created by the MTA as messages are queued and as reports are sent, received, and analysed, and are then available for inspection through the WebUI, the CLI, and the JMAP API. The existing pages under Reports cover report *emission and analysis*; this page covers access to the records themselves.

 出站訊息佇列以及入站和出站報告存檔是運行時管理對象，而非配置對象。它們由MTA在訊息入隊以及報告發送、接收和分析時創建，然後可透過 WebUI 、CLI和JMAP API進行查看。 報告下的現有頁面涵蓋報告的*產生和分析*；本頁涵蓋對記錄本身的存取。

## Outbound queue｜出站隊列

 Messages waiting for delivery are represented by the QueuedMessage object \(found in the WebUI under Management › Emails › Queued\). Each entry carries the envelope, per-recipient delivery status, the next retry and notification timestamps, and a reference to the stored message content. Administrators can list, inspect, requeue, and cancel queued messages through this object.

 等待投遞的郵件由 QueuedMessage物件表示（可在 WebUI 的「管理」&gt;「電子郵件」&gt;「已排隊」中找到）。每個條目包含信封、每個收件人的投遞狀態、下次重試和通知時間戳，以及對已儲存郵件內容的引用。管理員可以透過此物件列出、檢視、重新排隊和取消已排隊的郵件。

## Inbound reports｜入站報告

 Reports received from other organisations are stored for review and trend analysis. The relevant objects are:

 從其他組織收到的報告將被存儲，以供審查和趨勢分析。相關對象包括：

- ArfExternalReport \(found in the WebUI under Management › Reports › Inbox › ARF\): ARF feedback reports, including abuse complaints, fraud notifications, and authentication-failure reports received from external senders.
ArfExternalReport （在 WebUI 的「管理 › 報告 › 收件匣 › ARF 」下找到）： ARF回饋報告，包括從外部寄件者收到的濫用、詐欺通知和身分驗證失敗報告。
- DmarcExternalReport \(found in the WebUI under Management › Reports › Inbox › DMARC\): DMARC aggregate reports returned by remote receivers, summarising SPF and DKIM results for messages claiming to come from the local domains.
DmarcExternalReport （可在 WebUI 的「管理 › 報告 › 收件匣 › DMARC」下找到）：遠端接收器傳回的 DMARC 聚合報告，匯總聲稱來自本地域的訊息的 SPF 和 DKIM 結果。
- TlsExternalReport \(found in the WebUI under Management › Reports › Inbox › TLS\): TLS aggregate reports describing the outcome of STARTTLS connections observed by remote servers when delivering mail to the local domains.
TlsExternalReport （在 WebUI 的「管理」›「報告」 ›「收件匣」› TLS下找到）： TLS匯總報告，描述遠端伺服器在傳送郵件時觀察到本地域STARTTLS連接結果。
Inbound analysis of these reports is configured on the ReportSettings singleton; see Analysis for the intercept addresses and forwarding behaviour.
 這些報告的入站分析在 ReportSettings單例中配置；有關攔截位址和轉發行為，請參閱 Analysis 。

## Outbound reports｜出站報告

 Reports generated locally and waiting to be sent are retained until the aggregation window closes and delivery completes. The relevant objects are:

 本機產生且待發送的報告將保留至聚合視窗關閉且發送完成為止。相關對象包括：

- DmarcInternalReport \(found in the WebUI under Management › Reports › Outbox › DMARC\): pending outbound DMARC aggregate reports, accumulated from local authentication results and scheduled for delivery to the addresses listed in each domain’s DMARC policy.
DmarcInternalReport （在 WebUI 的「管理 › 報告 › 發件箱 › DMARC 」下找到）：待處理的出站DMARC匯總報告，從本地身份DMARC結果
- TlsInternalReport \(found in the WebUI under Management › Reports › Outbox › TLS\): pending outbound TLS aggregate reports, accumulated from observed STARTTLS outcomes and scheduled for delivery to the policy identifiers declared by remote domains.
TlsInternalReport （在 WebUI 的「管理 › 報告 › 寄件箱 › TLS 」下找到）：待處理的出站TLS匯總報告，從觀察到的STARTTLS域聲明中累積策略
Emission of outbound reports is configured through the DMARC and TLS report pages; this page covers only the management of records that are already in flight.
 出站報告的發出是透過 DMARC和 TLS報告頁面進行配置的；此頁面僅涵蓋已在處理中的記錄的管理。
