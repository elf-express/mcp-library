# POP3

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[migration｜遷移\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-37&gt;\\\) › \\\[proxy｜代理人\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-42&gt;\\\) › \\\[routing｜路由\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-46&gt;\\\)

The POP3 listener serves the implicit-TLS convention on port 995 and the STARTTLS convention on port 110, depending on the listener’s `tls` mode. Its behavior mirrors the IMAP listener, adapted to the POP3 command set.

 POP3監聽器依監聽器的`tls`模式，在連接埠 995 上提供隱式TLS約定，在連接埠 110 上提供STARTTLS約定。它的行為與 IMAP監聽器類似，並針對POP3指令集進行了調整。

## Pre-authentication｜預認證

 On connection the proxy sends a `+OK` greeting carrying the configured banner. Before authentication it handles `CAPA` \(returning the advertised capability list\), `NOOP`, `UTF8`, `QUIT`, and `STLS` to begin TLS negotiation on a STARTTLS listener. `APOP` is acknowledged as unsupported. The advertised capabilities come from `[capabilities.pop3]`; the `USER` capability and the SASL mechanism list are only advertised when cleartext authentication is permitted on the current connection, and `STLS` is advertised on a STARTTLS listener before the upgrade.

 連接時，代理程式會傳送帶有配置橫幅的`+OK`問候語。在驗證之前，它會處理`CAPA`（傳回通告的功能清單）、`NOOP`, `UTF8`, `QUIT`和`STLS`，以開始在STARTTLS偵聽器上進行TLS協商。 `APOP` 被確認為不受支援。宣傳的能力來自`[capabilities.pop3]`；`USER` 能力和SASL 機制清單僅在當前連接允許明文身份驗證時才會發布，`STLS` 在升級前在STARTTLS 監聽器上發布。

## Authentication｜驗證

 POP3 offers two authentication paths. The legacy `USER` and `PASS` command pair supplies the username and password across two commands; the proxy holds the `USER` value and, on receiving `PASS`, assembles the credentials and proceeds. The `AUTH` command carries a SASL mechanism, one of `PLAIN`, `LOGIN`, `OAUTHBEARER` or `XOAUTH2`, with the initial response supplied inline or after a continuation prompt; `AUTH` with no argument lists the available mechanisms. `AUTH LOGIN` is offered whenever `LOGIN` appears in the `sasl` list and cleartext authentication is permitted; the proxy issues the base64 `Username:` and `Password:` challenges itself and reconstructs the answers as a `PLAIN` challenge, so the backend leg only ever sees `PLAIN`.

 POP3 提供兩種身份驗證途徑。傳統 `USER` 和 `PASS` 命令對透過兩個命令提供使用者名稱和密碼；代理伺服器持有該命令。 `USER` 價值，以及在收到 `PASS`收集好憑證後繼續操作。 `AUTH` 命令攜帶一個 SASL 機制，其中之一 `PLAIN`, `LOGIN`, `OAUTHBEARER` 或者 `XOAUTH2`初始回應以內聯方式提供，或在繼續提示後提供； `AUTH` 沒有參數，則列出可用的機制。 `AUTH LOGIN` 隨時提供 `LOGIN` 出現在 `sasl` 允許清單和明文身份驗證；代理伺服器發出 base64 編碼。 `Username:` 和 `Password:` 挑戰自我，並將答案重構為… `PLAIN` 挑戰，因此後端腿只會看到 `PLAIN`.

 Cleartext authentication, meaning `USER`/`PASS`, `AUTH PLAIN` or `AUTH LOGIN`, is gated on transport security and is refused until the connection is encrypted unless `allow_plain_auth_without_tls` is set. The routing identifier is the supplied username, or the username carried in an OAuth frame, extracted as described under Routing.

 明文認證（即`USER`/`PASS`, `AUTH PLAIN`或`AUTH LOGIN` ）受傳輸安全機制的限制，除非設定了`allow_plain_auth_without_tls` ，否則在連接加密前將被拒絕。路由標識符是提供的用戶名，或OAuth幀中攜帶的用戶名，提取方式如路由中所述。

## Backend handshake｜後端握手

 The proxy connects to the destination’s POP3 endpoint and reads the backend’s `+OK` greeting. On a STARTTLS endpoint it issues `CAPA`, confirms the backend advertises `STLS`, performs the upgrade, and continues over the encrypted connection. It then verifies the backend leg is encrypted.

 代理連接到目標端的POP3端點，並讀取後端`+OK`的問候資訊。在STARTTLS端點上，它發出`CAPA`問候訊息，確認後端通告了`STLS` ，執行升級，並透過加密連線繼續通訊。然後，它驗證後端連結是否已加密。

 When the destination uses `XCLIENT` forwarding and the backend advertised `XCLIENT` in its greeting, the proxy issues an `XCLIENT` command conveying the client and destination addresses, a session identifier and the transport security before authenticating. It then replays the client’s authentication, relays the result, and bridges the session on success.

 當目標伺服器使用`XCLIENT`轉發，且後端伺服器在其歡迎訊息中通告了`XCLIENT`時，代理伺服器會在進行身份驗證之前發出`XCLIENT`命令，該命令會傳遞客戶端和目標伺服器的位址、會話標識符以及傳輸安全訊息。然後，代理伺服器會重播客戶端的身份驗證請求，轉發結果，並在成功後建立會話。
