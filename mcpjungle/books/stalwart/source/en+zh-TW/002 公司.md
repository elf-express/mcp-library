# The company｜公司

 About Stalwart Labs

 關於 Stalwart Labs

## The company behind Stalwart.｜Stalwart背後的公司。

 Stalwart Labs builds Stalwart, the open-source mail and collaboration server used by governments, universities, hosting providers, email service providers and enterprises of all sizes around the world.

 Stalwart Labs 開發了 Stalwart，這是一款開源郵件和協作伺服器，被世界各地的政府機構、大學、託管服務提供者、電子郵件服務提供者和各種規模的企業所使用。

 What we build

 我們建造

## A modern mail and collaboration server.｜一款現代化的郵件和協作伺服器。

 Stalwart is a single product that covers the mail and collaboration protocols end to end \(JMAP, IMAP, POP3, SMTP, CalDAV, CardDAV and WebDAV\), with a built-in spam and phishing filter and a configuration model that an administrator can read in one sitting. It is dual-licensed: a free Community edition under AGPL-3.0 and a commercial Enterprise edition that adds operational features for larger and multi-tenant deployments.

 Stalwart 是一款涵蓋端到端郵件和協作協議（ JMAP, IMAP, POP3, SMTP 、CalDAV、CardDAV 和 WebDAV）的單一產品，內建垃圾郵件和網路釣魚過濾器，其配置模型簡潔易懂，管理員只需閱讀一遍即可掌握。它採用雙重授權：免費的 社群版 （ AGPL-3.0和商業 企業版 ，後者為更大規模和多租戶部署增加了更多運維功能。

 What we're building toward

 我們正在朝什麼方向努力

## Replacing the legacy mail stack with one product.｜用單一產品取代原有的郵件系統。

 Most mail-server stacks in production still combine three or four separate components, each with its own configuration language and operational quirks. We started Stalwart Labs in response to that slow-moving market: rather than gluing together yet another stack, we set out to build the mail and collaboration server we wished existed, with security as a default and clustering designed in from the start. Our goal is to make running modern email reliable, secure and affordable, whether the operator is a single sysadmin or a hosting platform with millions of mailboxes.

 目前大多數生產環境中的郵件伺服器架構仍然由三到四個獨立元件組成，每個元件都有其自身的配置語言和操作特性。我們創立 Stalwart Labs 正是為了應對這種發展緩慢的市場現狀：我們沒有選擇拼湊出另一個現有的架構，而是著手建立我們理想中的郵件和協作伺服器，將安全性作為預設配置，並從一開始就將叢集功能融入設計之中。我們的目標是讓現代電子郵件的運作變得可靠、安全且經濟實惠，無論操作者是單一系統管理員還是擁有數百萬個郵箱的託管平台。

 Track record

 過往記錄

## Young product, broad adoption.｜產品年輕，但應用廣泛。

 Stalwart Labs was founded in July 2019. Work on the Stalwart server began in September 2021, and the first public release shipped in September 2022. Stalwart is the **most-starred mail server project on GitHub, with tens of thousands of stars, tens of thousands of production servers running worldwide, and millions of licenses issued to date. The product is younger than the team behind it: the founder spent more than three decades building email and enterprise software before starting the company, and the design choices in Stalwart reflect that. Stalwart is used by governments, universities, hosting providers, email service providers and enterprises of all sizes;**references are available on request.

 Stalwart Labs成立於2019年7月。 Stalwart伺服器的開發工作於2021年9月啟動，首個公開版本於2022年9月發布。 Stalwart是GitHub上**星標數最多的郵件伺服器專案，擁有數萬顆星標，全球數萬台生產伺服器正在運行，迄今已頒發數百萬份許可證。這款產品比背後的團隊更年輕：創辦人在創辦公司之前，擁有超過三十年的電子郵件和企業軟體開發經驗，Stalwart的設計選擇也體現了這一點。 Stalwart的使用者包括政府機構、大學、託管服務提供者、電子郵件服務提供者以及各種規模的企業；**如有需要，可提供相關案例。

 The team

 團隊

## A distributed team behind a global product.｜一個分散式的團隊打造全球產品。

 Stalwart Labs is incorporated in both the United States and the United Kingdom. The team works remotely, distributed across multiple time zones, so support and development cover the hours our customers operate in. The combined experience behind the product spans email, security, large-scale storage and compliance, accumulated across decades of building enterprise software.

 Stalwart Labs 在美國和英國均設有註冊公司。團隊成員遠距辦公，分佈在多個時區，因此能夠確保支援和開發工作涵蓋客戶的營運時間。產品背後的團隊擁有豐富的經驗，涵蓋電子郵件、安全、大規模儲存和合規性等領域，這些經驗源自於數十年來建構企業軟體的實踐。
