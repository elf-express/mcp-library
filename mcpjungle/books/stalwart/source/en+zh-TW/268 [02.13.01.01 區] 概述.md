# Overview｜概述

> 章節：\\\[docs｜文件\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-2&gt;\\\) › \\\[mta｜捷運\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-47&gt;\\\) › \\\[authentication｜驗證\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-48&gt;\\\) › \\\[dkim｜區\\\]\\\(&lt;000 目錄.md#c-49&gt;\\\)

DomainKeys Identified Mail \(DKIM\) is an email-authentication method that verifies the authenticity of an email message using public-key cryptography. A digital signature is added to the message header; the signature can be verified against a public key published in DNS under the sending domain. DKIM prevents email spoofing and allows recipients to confirm that a message genuinely originated from the claimed domain.

 網域金鑰識別郵件 \(DKIM\) 是一種電子郵件驗證方法，使用公鑰加密技術驗證電子郵件的真實性。在訊息頭中加入數位簽章；可以根據發送域下DNS中發布的公鑰來驗證簽章。 DKIM 防止電子郵件欺騙，並允許收件者確認郵件確實源自所聲明的網域。

 With DKIM enabled, outgoing messages carry one or more signatures that help build sender reputation, reduce the chance of legitimate messages being marked as spam, and improve overall email security.

 啟用 DKIM 後，外寄郵件會攜帶一個或多個簽名，有助於建立寄件者信譽、減少合法郵件被標記為垃圾郵件的機會，並提高整體電子郵件安全性。

 DKIM signatures are defined as DkimSignature objects \(found in the WebUI under Management › Domains › DKIM Signatures\). Each signature is a multi-variant object: the `Dkim1Ed25519Sha256` variant is used for Ed25519 signatures and the `Dkim1RsaSha256` variant for RSA signatures. See the signing and verifying pages for details.

 DKIM 簽章定義為 DkimSignature 物件（可在 WebUI 的管理 › 網域 › DKIM 簽章下找到）。每個簽名都是多變體物件：`Dkim1Ed25519Sha256`變體用於Ed25519簽名，`Dkim1RsaSha256`變體用於RSA簽名。詳情請參閱簽名和驗證頁面。
