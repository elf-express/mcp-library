## About OSI｜關於OSI

“We are the stewards of the Open Source Definition (OSD) and the community-recognized body for reviewing and approving licenses as OSD-conformant.”

“我們是開源定義 (OSD) 的管理者，也是社區認可的負責審查和批准OSD合規許可證的機構。”

*source:* [opensource.org](http://opensource.org/about)

*資料來源：* [opensource.org](http://opensource.org/about)

## Relation to OPNsense｜與 OPNsense 的關係

OPNsense is licensed under an Open Source Initiative [approved license](http://opensource.org/licenses). OPNsense is and will be available with the simple 2-clause BSD license. We believe an open source project should provide the sources and the tools to build it.

OPNsense 根據開源計劃 [批准的許可證](http://opensource.org/licenses) 獲得許可。 OPNsense 現已且將透過簡單的 2 條款 BSD 授權提供。我們相信開源專案應該提供建構它的原始碼和工具。

## Deciso B.V.｜德西索 B.V.

As founder, trademark owner and largest sponsor of OPNsense, Deciso is an active sponsor of the Open Source Initiative. see [OSI Corporate Sponsors & Support](http://opensource.org/sponsors)

作為 OPNsense 的創始人、商標所有者和最大贊助商，Deciso 是開源計劃的積極贊助商。請參閱[OSI企業贊助與支持](http://opensource.org/sponsors)

Deciso believes that sharing knowledge makes for better products.

Deciso 相信分享知識可以創造更好的產品。
