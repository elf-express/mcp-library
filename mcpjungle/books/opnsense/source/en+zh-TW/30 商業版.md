# Business Edition｜商業版

> 章節：[Releases](<000 目錄.md#c-1>) › [Business Edition](<000 目錄.md#c-3>)

[![_images/architecture-blue-sky-business-2599538.jpg](<../images/70622649-architecture-blue-sky-business-2599538.jpg>)](https://docs.opnsense.org/_images/architecture-blue-sky-business-2599538.jpg)

OPNsense Business Edition is intended for companies, enterprises and professionals looking for a more selective upgrade path (lags behind the community edition), additional commercial features and who want to support the project in a more commercial way compared to donating.

OPNsense 商業版是針對希望獲得更具選擇性的升級路徑（落後於社群版）、更多商業功能，並希望以比捐贈更商業化的方式支持該專案的公司、企業和專業人士。
