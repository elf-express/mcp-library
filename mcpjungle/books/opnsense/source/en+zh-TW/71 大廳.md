# Lobby｜大廳

> 章節：[Lobby](<000 目錄.md#c-8>)

[![_images/architecture-building-ceiling-lamp-260931.jpg](<../images/1c622abb-architecture-building-ceiling-lamp-26093.jpg>)](https://docs.opnsense.org/_images/architecture-building-ceiling-lamp-260931.jpg)

The lobby is the entrance to your (virtual) security appliance, where you can find your dashboard, change your password and end your session. After initial installation, this is the first place you will visit.

大廳是您（虛擬）安全設備的入口，您可以在這裡找到控制台、更改密碼和結束會話。首次安裝後，這是您首先會訪問的地方。
