# 16.1 “Crafty Coyote”（狡猾的郊狼） Series｜16.1 “Crafty Coyote”（狡猾的郊狼）系列

> 章節：[Releases](<000 目錄.md#c-1>) › [Community Edition](<000 目錄.md#c-2>)

No, we would not say it was easy getting here, but booting into 16.1 for the first time sure is as relieving (and exciting) as it could get for our project growing beyond what we had ever imagined. It has been more than a year since OPNsense first came out. Back then it was FreeBSD 10.0. Not even two months after, 10.1 was introduced along with the opnsense-update utility. Today is the day for FreeBSD 10.2, the latest and greatest release currently available for broader driver support and stability improvements.

不，我們不會說到達這裡很容易，但是第一次啟動16.1肯定是令人放鬆（並且令人興奮）的，因為我們的項目發展超出了我們的想像。自 OPNsense 首次問世以來已經一年多了。那時是 FreeBSD 10.0。不到兩個月後，10.1 與 opnsense-update 實用程式一起推出。今天是 FreeBSD 10.2 的日子，它是目前可用於更廣泛的驅動程式支援和穩定性改進的最新、最強大的版本。

16.1 is nicknamed “Crafty Coyote”（狡猾的郊狼） in honour of our beloved childhood TV sessions. It is the accumulation of 6 months of work, having had our focus on reengineering the captive portal, native intrusion prevention, plugin support, and transforming the reporting frontend into something more modern and flexible just to name a few [\[1\]](https://opnsense.org/about/road-map/) . Apart from the recently published security advisories (see patch notes below), we have included a quick navigation feature which can be activated by pressing (TAB) followed by search keywords and hitting (ENTER) to go to the desired page. Last but not least, a larger batch of improvements and fixes went into assorted sections of the GUI that certainly help to get your work done without ending up dazed and confused.

16.1 被暱稱為 “Crafty Coyote”（狡猾的郊狼），以紀念我們摯愛的童年 TV 課程。這是 6 個月工作的積累，我們的重點是重新設計強制門戶、本機入侵防護、插件支持，並將報告前端轉變為更現代、更靈活的東西，僅舉幾例 [\[1\]](https://opnsense.org/about/road-map/)。除了最近發布的安全公告（請參閱下面的補丁說明）之外，我們還提供了快速導航功能，可以透過按（TAB）然後搜尋關鍵字並點擊（ENTER）來啟動該功能以前往所需的頁面。最後但並非最不重要的一點是，對GUI的各個部分進行了大量的改進和修復，這肯定有助於您完成工作，而不會感到頭暈和困惑。

Speaking of clearing things up, there is more… While Ad, Franco and a couple of amazing external contributors have been busy writing and reviewing code, Jos worked in the shadows to bring to you a fully revised set of project documentation in the form of an online handbook [\[2\]](https://docs.opnsense.org/) . More content will follow as we slow down development speed a bit in order to catch up. We will have to see how that works out. ;)

說到清理工作，還有更多…當 Ad、Franco 和一些出色的外部貢獻者一直忙於編寫和審查程式碼時，Jos 在幕後工作，以線上手冊 [\[2\]](https://docs.opnsense.org/) 的形式為您帶來一套經過全面修訂的專案文件。隨著我們稍微放慢開發速度以趕上，更多內容將會隨之而來。我們必須看看結果如何。 ;)

Another thing we have noticed is that translations are hard! We have planned to finish a translation for this iteration, but the sheer amount of work overwhelmed even the sizeable German translation team. The German translation is now at 77% percent completed with Japanese, Chinese and French chasing tails. If you want to help drop us a line at [project@opnsense.org](mailto:project%40opnsense.org) for details on how to contribute.

我們注意到的另一件事是翻譯很困難！我們計劃完成這次迭代的翻譯，但龐大的工作量甚至讓規模龐大的德語翻譯團隊不堪重負。德文翻譯目前已完成 77%，日文、中文和法文正在緊追不捨。如果您想提供協助，請發送電子郵件至 [project@opnsense.org](mailto:project%40opnsense.org)，以了解有關如何貢獻的詳細資訊。

All images have been pushed as well, although may take a bit more time to reach a mirror near you. You can find the checksums attached at the end of this announcement.

所有圖像也已推送，儘管可能需要更多時間才能到達您附近的鏡子。您可以在本公告末尾找到所附的校驗和。

[https://opnsense.org/download/](https://opnsense.org/download/)

## 16.1.16 (June 06, 2016)｜16.1.16（2016 年 6 月 6 日）

It has been a long journey for HardenedBSD and OPNsense, and finally the paths start to merge as the splendid and battle- proven ASLR implementation gets incorporated into the default installation! It is just the beginning as we will start to leverage the extra security by enabling position independent execution in 16.7 and merge more security-related features. We thank again the HardenedBSD team for their continued efforts on making this world a safer place.

對於 HardenedBSD 和 OPNsense 來說，這是一個漫長的旅程，隨著出色且久經考驗的 ASLR 實現被納入預設安裝，最終路徑開始合併！這只是一個開始，我們將開始透過在 16.7 中啟用位置獨立執行來利用額外的安全性，並合併更多與安全相關的功能。我們再次感謝 HardenedBSD 團隊為讓這個世界更安全所做的持續努力。

In other news, there is a thoroughly revamped dashboard for you to enjoy and a handful of security fixes in FreeBSD and the ports ecosystem. LibreSSL has been updated to the latest production release and the BETA version is progressing nicely as we change our working mode from “rework all the things” to “polish all the things”. A release candidate is coming up soon.

Today is the day for FreeBSD 10.2, the latest and greatest release currently available for broader driver support and stability improvements.

不，我們不會說走到今天這一步很容易，但第一次啟動16.1確實令人如釋重負（也激動不已），我們的專案發展速度遠遠超出了我們的想像。 OPNsense 首次發布至今已超過一年。當時的版本是 FreeBSD 10.0 。不到兩個月後， 10.1和 opnsense-update 工具一同發布。今天，FreeBSD 10.2正式發布，這是目前最新、最強大的版本，旨在提供更廣泛的驅動程式支援並提升穩定性。

16.1 is nicknamed “Crafty Coyote”（狡猾的郊狼） in honour of our beloved childhood TV sessions. It is the accumulation of 6 months of work, having had our focus on reengineering the captive portal, native intrusion prevention, plugin support, and transforming the reporting frontend into something more modern and flexible just to name a few [\[1\]](https://opnsense.org/about/road-map/) . Apart from the recently published security advisories (see patch notes below), we have included a quick navigation feature which can be activated by pressing (TAB) followed by search keywords and hitting (ENTER) to go to the desired page. Last but not least, a larger batch of improvements and fixes went into assorted sections of the GUI that certainly help to get your work done without ending up dazed and confused.

16.1暱稱是“Crafty Coyote”（狡猾的郊狼）以此紀念我們摯愛的童年時光TV 。它是我們歷時六個月努力的成果，期間我們專注於重構強制門戶、原生入侵防禦、插件支持，並將報告前端改造得更加現代化和靈活，等等[\[1\]]( https://opnsense.org/about/road-map/ )。除了最近發布的安全公告（請參閱下方修補程式說明）之外，我們還新增了快速導航功能，只需按下 ( TAB )，然後輸入搜尋關鍵字，再按下 ( ENTER ) 即可跳到所需頁面。最後， GUI中的各個部分也進行了大量改進和修復，這些改進和修復無疑將幫助您更有效率地完成工作，避免感到困惑和不知所措。

Speaking of clearing things up, there is more… While Ad, Franco and a couple of amazing external contributors have been busy writing and reviewing code, Jos worked in the shadows to bring to you a fully revised set of project documentation in the form of an online handbook [\[2\]](https://docs.opnsense.org/) . More content will follow as we slow down development speed a bit in order to catch up. We will have to see how that works out. ;)

說到澄清一些事情，還有更多……在 Ad、Franco 和幾位優秀的外部貢獻者忙於編寫和審查代碼的同時，Jos 則在幕後默默工作，為大家帶來了一套全面修訂的項目文檔，以在線手冊的形式呈現 [\[2\]]( https://docs.opnsense.org/ )。為了趕上進度，我們會稍微放慢開發速度，之後會陸續發布更多內容。讓我們拭目以待吧。 ;)

Another thing we have noticed is that translations are hard! We have planned to finish a translation for this iteration, but the sheer amount of work overwhelmed even the sizeable German translation team. The German translation is now at 77% percent completed with Japanese, Chinese and French chasing tails. If you want to help drop us a line at [project@opnsense.org](mailto:project%40opnsense.org) for details on how to contribute.

我們也注意到，翻譯工作非常困難！我們原本計劃完成本版本的所有翻譯工作，但工作量之大，即使是規模龐大的德語翻譯團隊也難以應付。目前德文翻譯已完成 77%，而日文、中文和法文翻譯則進展緩慢。如果您想提供協助，請發送電子郵件至 [project@opnsense.org](mailto:project%40opnsense.org)以了解具體貢獻方式。

All images have been pushed as well, although may take a bit more time to reach a mirror near you. You can find the checksums attached at the end of this announcement.

所有圖片均已上傳，但可能需要一些時間才能到達您附近的鏡像來源。您可以在本公告末尾找到校驗和。
