# System｜系統

> 章節：[System](<000 目錄.md#c-11>)

[![_images/control-data-device-270700.jpg](<../images/dc35c2f4-control-data-device-270700.jpg>)](https://docs.opnsense.org/_images/control-data-device-270700.jpg)

The system section in the menu houses all general settings for your firewall needed for its operation. This includes options like administrative access, network routing and diagnostics features to debug your devices current activities.

選單中的“系統”部分包含防火牆運作所需的所有常規設定。這包括管理存取權限、網路路由和診斷功能等選項，用於調試設備的當前活動。
