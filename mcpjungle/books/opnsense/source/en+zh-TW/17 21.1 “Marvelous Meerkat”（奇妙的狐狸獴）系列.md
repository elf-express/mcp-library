# 21.1 “Marvelous Meerkat”（奇妙的狐狸獴） Series｜21.1 “Marvelous Meerkat”（奇妙的狐狸獴）系列

> 章節：[Releases](<000 目錄.md#c-1>) › [Community Edition](<000 目錄.md#c-2>)

For more than 6 years, OPNsense is driving innovation through modularising and hardening the open source firewall, with simple and reliable firmware upgrades, multi-language support, HardenedBSD security, fast adoption of upstream software updates as well as clear and stable 2-Clause BSD licensing.

6 年多來，OPNsense 透過模組化和強化開源防火牆來推動創新，提供簡單可靠的韌體升級、多語言支援、強化的 BSD 安全性、快速採用上游軟體更新以及清晰穩定的 2 條款BSD許可。

21.1, nicknamed “Marvelous Meerkat”（奇妙的狐狸獴）, is the relentless continuation of open source dedication. The last 6 years were not always easy, but we are happy to be where we are now and have the community to thank for it.

21.1 ，又稱“Marvelous Meerkat”（奇妙的狐狸獴） ，是對開源奉獻精神的不懈延續。過去的六年並非一帆風順，但我們很高興能取得今天的成就，並衷心感謝社區的支持。

New and improved are the firewall rules and NAT categories, the traffic graphs supporting IPv6 along with a visual refresh, intrusion detection rule management by policies, an alias for MAC addresses and NAT over IPsec with all phase 2 you could ever want. Last but not least, the serial image now supports UEFI as well.

新增和改進的功能包括防火牆規則和NAT類別、支援IPv6的流量圖表（並進行了視覺刷新）、基於策略的入侵檢測規則管理、 MAC地址別名以及支援所有您可能需要的基於IPsec的NAT第二階段功能。最後，串行鏡像現在也支援UEFI 。

For those wondering, the WireGuard plugin has been available since 2019 and receives continuous improvements by its maintainer and various users alike. And that is unlikely to change in the future. ;)

如果你好奇的話，WireGuard 外掛自 2019 年發布以來，一直由其維護者和眾多用戶持續改進。而且這種情況在未來不太可能改變。 ;)

As we continue to deprecate custom configuration inputs for a number of reasons, Dnsmasq has been switched to a pluggable file-based approach [\[1\]](<194 Dnsmasq DNS & DHCP.md>) with Unbound to follow in the upcoming 21.7 series.

由於多種原因，我們繼續棄用自訂設定輸入，Dnsmasq 已切換到可插拔的基於文件的方法 [\[1\]]( https://docs.opnsense.org/manual/dnsmasq.html )，Unbound 將在即將到來的21.7系列中跟進。
