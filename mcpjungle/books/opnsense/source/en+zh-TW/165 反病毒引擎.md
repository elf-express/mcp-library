# Anti Virus Engine｜反病毒引擎

> 章節：[Community Plugins](<000 目錄.md#c-36>) › [Web](<000 目錄.md#c-37>)

[![../_images/eye_on_virus_new.jpg](<../images/318a0696-eye_on_virus_new.jpg>)](https://docs.opnsense.org/_images/eye_on_virus_new.jpg)

**OPNsense** offers the industry standard ICAP to protect HTTP and HTTPS connections against ransomware, trojans, viruses and other malware .

**OPNsense** 提供業界標準的ICAP來保護HTTP和HTTPS連接免受勒索軟體、木馬、病毒和其他惡意軟體的侵害。

OPNsense offers a ClamAV plugin, which can be used with the C-ICAP plugin or relies on third party engines from well known vendors, such as Symantec’s Protection Engine.

OPNsense 提供了一個 ClamAV 插件，該插件可以與 C- ICAP插件一起使用，或依賴來自知名供應商的第三方引擎，例如 Symantec 的 Protection Engine。
