## About｜關於

[Deciso](https://www.deciso.com/) is a globally operating manufacturer of network equipment and a highly innovative company that develops network appliances and middleware software.

[Deciso](https://www.deciso.com/)是一家全球營運的網路設備製造商，也是一家開發網路設備和中介軟體的高度創新公司。

Our field of expertise ranges from open source firewall & utm technology to telecommunications and business intelligence. The company was founded in 2000 with a strong focus on open source technology.

我們的專業領域涵蓋從開源防火牆和 utm 技術到電信和商業智慧。該公司成立於 2000 年，專注於開源技術。

**Free, Libre Open Source Software**

**免費、自由的開源軟體**

Open source software makes development easier, faster, it makes you less depended on the supplier and results in lower cost of ownership. As a true believer Deciso use open source software all around from our back-end systems to the turn-key appliances that you can acquire from our resellers. Deciso is an active sponsor of the Open Source Initiative (OSI) and founder of the open source OPNsense firewall software.

開源軟體使開發變得更容易、更快，它使您減少對供應商的依賴，並降低擁有成本。作為一個真正的信徒，Deciso 全面使用開源軟體，從我們的後端系統到您可以從我們的經銷商購買的交鑰匙設備。 Deciso 是開源倡議 (OSI) 的積極贊助者，也是開源 OPNsense 防火牆軟體的創始人。

## Relations with OPNsense｜與 OPNsense 的關係

Deciso founded OPNsense and offers the project a stable environment. The company has a long history in providing networking solutions using open source software and has supported many OSS projects in the past.

Deciso 創立了 OPNsense 並為該專案提供了穩定的環境。該公司在使用開源軟體提供網路解決方案方面擁有悠久的歷史，並且過去支持過許多OSS專案。

Our extensive knowledge of software development provides the OPNsense community with a modern and professional approach and results in maintainable software.

我們在軟體開發方面的豐富知識為 OPNsense 社群提供了現代且專業的方法，並產生了可維護的軟體。

In its effort to create the most widely used open source security platform, Deciso has been joined by multiple partners who share the same views on open and transparent software.

為了創建使用最廣泛的開源安全平台，Deciso 得到了多個對開放和透明軟體持有相同看法的合作夥伴的加入。
